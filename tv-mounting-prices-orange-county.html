FAQ

TV Mounting Cost Questions & Answers

How much does TV mounting cost in Orange County, NY?
+

TV mounting in Orange County starts at $229.99 for a 32″–55″ TV on drywall.

Do you mount TVs in Newburgh and Middletown?
+

Yes, along with Monroe, Goshen, Warwick, Cornwall and Chester.

How much does it cost to mount a TV?
+

In Orange County, NY, mounting a 32″–55″ TV on drywall costs $229.99, labor included. Larger TVs, harder walls, fireplace mounts and hidden wires add set amounts listed on this page.

How much would someone charge to hang a 65 inch TV?
+

$259.99 in Orange County, NY on a flat or tilt mount on drywall, plus the bracket if you need one.

How much does it cost to mount a TV above a fireplace?
+

The fireplace adds $130 to the base price (pull-down mounts add $160). Brick or stone adds $65. For a 65″ TV over a brick fireplace in Orange County, NY that’s $454.99 before the bracket.

Is it safe to mount a TV above a fireplace?
+

Usually, if the mantel blocks rising heat and the wall above stays comfortable to touch with the fire on. The bigger issue is height — a pull-down mount fixes the neck-strain problem.

How much does it cost to hide TV wires?
+

In-wall concealment with a code-compliant power and cable kit is $169. A paintable on-wall cord cover is $59.

How high should a TV be mounted?
+

For most living rooms, put the center of the screen roughly at seated eye level — about 40–45 inches from the floor. For a bedroom TV watched from bed, a little higher with a tilting mount.

What size TV mount do I need?
+

Match the mount to your TV’s VESA pattern (the bolt spacing on the back), its size and its weight. Check the TV’s manual or tell us the model and we’ll pick a bracket that fits.

Do TV installers provide mounting brackets?
+

We sell Sanus, MantelMount and SunBrite brackets at list price, or install yours if it’s rated for the TV’s size and weight.

How long does TV mounting take?
+

Most single-TV installs take 1 to 2 hours. Fireplace mounts, in-wall wire hiding and masonry walls take longer.

Can one person mount a 75 inch TV?
+

One of our technicians handles up to 85″. For 86″ and bigger we send two.

Can a landlord prohibit TV mounting?
+

Yes — many leases restrict drilling, especially into brick. Check your lease or ask your landlord first. We don’t patch or paint holes when a TV comes down.

Can a mounted TV damage the wall?
+

Mounting leaves bolt holes behind the TV. Done right, the wall isn’t damaged otherwise. A TV pulling away from the wall is almost always the wrong anchors — we remount those.

Can someone mount my TV this weekend?
+

Yes, we work 7 days a week, 8 AM to 8 PM. Pick an open 2-hour window when you book.

Can someone move my mounted TV to another room?
+

Yes — take-down is $85 and old bracket removal $45, then the new mount is priced like any install.

Is there a company that mounts TVs and soundbars?
+

Yes, us. Mounting a soundbar under the TV and connecting it is $129, or $79 placed on a stand.

Who can install TVs in my restaurant?
+

We do restaurant, bar and sports bar TV installation — ceiling and wall mounts, multiple TVs and menu boards — with a COI for your landlord. Multi-TV jobs get a written quote.

Who can mount multiple TVs in my office?
+

We mount conference room, lobby and waiting room TVs and digital signage for offices, schools, gyms and medical offices, with cable runs and network connections.

Who installs really big TVs?
+

We do. One technician handles up to 85″; for 86″, 98″ and 100″ TVs we send two, with the right mount and a backer board when the wall needs it.

Do I need an electrician to install an outlet behind my TV?
+

Only for a brand-new electrical outlet. Most people don’t need one: a code-compliant in-wall power and cable kit ($169) brings power behind the TV from the outlet below. We’re low-voltage and don’t do electrical work.

What is the difference between a fixed and full motion mount?
+

A fixed (low-profile) mount holds the TV flat against the wall. A full-motion mount lets you pull it out, swivel and angle it — it adds $40 on our list.

Should I hire a professional to mount my TV?
+

If your wall is anything other than drywall over wood studs, the TV is over 65″, or it’s going over a fireplace, yes.

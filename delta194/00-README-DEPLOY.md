## Distinct theses

**Jerome Park** — the wiring is sound and the map is not. Reservoir-facing Art Deco elevator stock where the original installations were competent and what has drifted is the correspondence between the directory plate and the building. Directory reconciliation treated as its own defined scope rather than something done incidentally.

**Kingsbridge Heights** — the buildings were fitted to the hill. Split-level entries, stepped risers, and two measurable consequences: longer runs meaning voltage drop at the furthest station, and exposed vestibules meaning corroded contacts and fatigued door-frame crossings.

**Laconia** — small buildings are a different trade, not a scaled-down apartment job. Two and three family houses where the owner usually lives behind one of the doors, the authority question mostly evaporates, and the fault concentrates in wiring added when the house was converted.

How Hudson Valley Solar Trailer Pricing Is Set

Hudson Valley pricing reflects two real factors: tow distance from our Bronx GBP at 460 E Fordham Rd (30 minutes to Westchester, 110+ minutes to Ulster), and the LPC/ARB handling profile in each county. Westchester and Rockland anchor the Hudson Valley at $3,125 for the Entry tier. Orange and Putnam tier to $3,250–$3,525. Dutchess and Ulster — farthest north — top out at $3,825. ARB-district handling in Beacon, Rhinebeck, Kingston Stockade, Woodstock, and other historic districts is included at no upcharge. NDAA fleet for federal-funded projects (large federal- and corporate-funded sites) is available across all six counties with signed component manifests at delivery.

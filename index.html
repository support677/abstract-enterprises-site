What People Are Asking

Security Camera Installation in NYC: Your Questions Answered

These are the real questions New York homeowners, landlords, property managers, and business owners are asking on Google, Reddit, and forums about security camera installation. We answer every one.

How much does security camera installation cost in NYC?

A 4-camera residential security camera system with professional installation in New York City typically costs $850 to $1,500 for an apartment, condo, or small home. Larger properties and commercial CCTV systems run $1,500 to $5,000+ depending on the number of cameras, building complexity, and whether intercom or access control is included. At Abstract Enterprises, our 4-camera packages start at $850 with DVR/NVR, monitor, wiring, and installation included — no hidden fees, no monthly charges. Call (800) 486-0943 for a free on-site estimate.

Can you install security cameras in a NYC co-op or condo building?

Yes. We provide board-ready documentation including installation plans, equipment specifications, and wiring diagrams. We coordinate with building management, supers, and co-op/condo boards for access to common areas, risers, and infrastructure. We have experience with co-op alteration agreements and condo association rules across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and the Bronx. Our technicians minimize disruption and leave zero mess.

Are wired or wireless security cameras better for NYC apartments and buildings?

Wired PoE (Power-over-Ethernet) cameras are significantly more reliable in New York City buildings. The city’s construction is dominated by steel-frame high-rises, brick and concrete pre-war buildings, and thick plaster walls that block WiFi signals between floors and rooms. Wireless cameras frequently lose connection, drop frames, and fail when you need them most. Hardwired PoE cameras receive both power and data through a single Ethernet cable, delivering stable 4K video 24/7 regardless of wall thickness or WiFi congestion.

How do I prevent package theft in my NYC apartment lobby?

Package theft in NYC apartment lobbies is one of the top reasons building managers and supers call us. A visible security camera at the lobby entrance and delivery area is a proven crime deterrent. Our active deterrent cameras trigger built-in sirens and two-way audio when motion is detected, letting you address the situation from your phone in real time. For non-doorman buildings, we combine lobby cameras with a video intercom so residents can see who’s at the door and buzz in verified delivery drivers.

Can I watch my NYC security cameras from my phone?

Every system we install includes full remote video monitoring from your smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer. Pull up live camera feeds from anywhere in the world, review recorded footage, get push notifications when motion is detected, and talk through your camera’s two-way audio. The NVR records 24/7 to a local hard drive — you don’t need a cloud subscription or monthly monitoring fee to use remote access.

What security camera system is best for a NYC retail store or restaurant?

NYC restaurants and retail stores need cameras covering the entrance, cash register, kitchen or stockroom, sales floor, and rear exit. We recommend dome cameras for indoor commercial use — they’re low-profile, vandal-resistant, and blend into drop ceilings. For loss prevention, 4K resolution is critical for capturing facial details and reading license plates. Our systems record locally with no monthly fees, and you can monitor all feeds from your phone whether you’re on-site or off.

Do I need a permit to install security cameras in New York City?

In most cases, no permit is required for standard residential or commercial security camera installation in New York City. However, New York State has a two-party consent law for audio recording, and cameras cannot be pointed into a neighbor’s private space. In NYC landmark districts — which cover large areas of Manhattan, Brooklyn, and the Bronx — exterior mounting may require Landmarks Preservation Commission approval. Our technicians understand all local regulations and install code-compliant systems.

How many cameras does a typical NYC property need?

A typical NYC apartment needs 2 to 4 cameras covering the front door, hallway, and windows. Brownstones and townhouses usually need 4 to 8 cameras for the stoop, rear yard, side passage, and hallways. Multi-unit buildings need 8 to 32+ covering lobbies, hallways, stairwells, elevators, parking, and the building perimeter. Suburban homes on Long Island and in Westchester typically need 6 to 10 cameras to cover driveways, backyards, side yards, and garages. We map exact placement during the free on-site security assessment.

Is there a monthly fee or do I own the security camera system?

You own it outright. We install a local NVR (Network Video Recorder) that stores all footage on an on-site hard drive — no cloud subscription, no monthly monitoring contract, no recurring charges of any kind. You pay once for the equipment and installation, and the system is yours forever. Remote smartphone access is free and included. This is one of the biggest reasons New York property owners choose us over subscription-based security companies like ADT or Ring.

Can security cameras record at night in NYC?

Yes. Every outdoor camera we install has infrared night vision that captures clear black-and-white footage in complete darkness — up to 100 to 120 feet depending on the model. Our premium models include full-color night vision using a built-in warm LED light, so you get color footage even at night. This is especially important for NYC rear yards, alleys, basement entrances, parking garages, loading docks, and poorly lit areas where most property crime occurs after dark.

Can you install an intercom and security cameras at the same time?

Absolutely — and we recommend it. Installing intercom and security cameras together saves money on labor and wiring because both systems use similar low-voltage infrastructure. We install video intercom systems, key fob access control, smart doorbells, and elevator access control alongside security camera systems for NYC apartment buildings, co-ops, condos, commercial properties, and suburban homes. One installer, one visit, one integrated building security system.

What should I look for when hiring a security camera installer in NYC?

Look for a company that is licensed and insured in New York, has experience with your building type (high-rises, brownstones, co-ops, commercial), does a free on-site assessment before quoting, uses PoE wired systems instead of cheap wireless cameras, doesn’t lock you into a monthly monitoring contract, provides a warranty, and cleans up after the installation. Ask to see examples of their cable routing — exposed wires stapled across walls is a sign of amateur work. Abstract Enterprises checks every box.

How long does a typical security camera installation take in NYC?

Most NYC residential installations take 4 to 8 hours for a 4- to 8-camera system. Co-op and condo installations may take longer due to building access coordination. Commercial installations for retail stores and restaurants typically take 4 to 8 hours. Large multi-unit buildings and commercial projects may span 1 to 3 days. We schedule around your availability and complete everything in one visit whenever possible — cameras mounted, wired, NVR configured, and remote access set up on your phone before we leave.

Can I upgrade my existing NYC security cameras to HD or 4K?

Yes. If your home or business has an existing analog CCTV system with coaxial cable, we can upgrade to HD analog cameras that deliver much better resolution without running new wire. For full 4K IP quality, new Cat5e/Cat6 Ethernet cable is needed — but we often reuse existing cable paths through your walls, ceilings, and basements. We evaluate your current wiring during the free on-site assessment and recommend the most cost-effective upgrade path.

What brands of security cameras does Abstract Enterprises install?

We install professional-grade security cameras from leading manufacturers including Hikvision, Dahua, Uniview, and other commercial-grade brands built for reliability, weatherproofing, and smart detection. We do not install consumer brands like Ring, Nest, or Arlo — those are designed for DIY and lack the durability, resolution, and NVR integration that New York homeowners and businesses need. Every camera we install is IP66 or IP67 rated for outdoor use in all NYC weather conditions.

Can you install security cameras on a NYC brownstone without damaging the brick?

Yes. Our technicians specialize in brownstone and brick installations throughout New York City. We use masonry-specific anchoring designed for brick and limestone facades. Cables are routed through interior walls, basements, and existing conduit — not strapped across the front of your building. When exterior cable runs are unavoidable, we use color-matched covers that blend with the facade. We work in designated historic districts including Fort Greene, Cobble Hill, Brooklyn Heights, Park Slope, and Harlem without damaging or altering protected architecture.

Is it worth getting security cameras for my NYC home or business?

A professionally installed security camera system is one of the highest-ROI investments a New York property owner can make. Visible cameras deter crime before it happens, 24/7 recorded footage provides evidence for police reports and insurance claims, remote monitoring lets you check your property from anywhere, and the system pays for itself the first time it prevents a break-in, theft, or false liability claim. For businesses, cameras reduce employee theft, improve accountability, and can lower insurance premiums. For landlords, cameras protect common areas, reduce vandalism, and increase tenant satisfaction.

Do you install cameras in suburban areas outside NYC?

Yes. We serve all five NYC boroughs plus Long Island and the Hudson Valley. Suburban homes have different security needs than city apartments — larger lots with driveways, backyards, pools, garages, and multiple entry points require more cameras and longer cable runs. Our technicians route cables through attics, basements, soffits, and garage spaces for clean, invisible wiring on every type of suburban home including colonials, split-levels, ranches, capes, and estates.

Frequently Asked Questions

NYC Security Camera Questions Answered

How much does security camera installation cost in NYC?

+

Residential systems start at $850 for 4 cameras with NVR, monitor, and professional installation. Commercial systems run $1,500 to $5,000+. Call (800) 486-0943 for a free estimate based on an on-site assessment.

Can you install cameras in a NYC co-op or condo?

+

Yes. We provide board-ready documentation and coordinate with building management and supers for access to common areas and infrastructure.

Wired or wireless for NYC buildings?

+

Wired PoE is far more reliable. Steel-frame, brick, and concrete construction blocks WiFi signals. Hardwired PoE delivers stable 4K video through any building material.

Can I view my cameras from my phone?

+

Yes. Every system includes remote viewing via smartphone, tablet, or desktop with live feeds, recorded footage playback, and motion detection alerts. Free — no monthly charge.

How do I prevent package theft in my apartment lobby?

+

Visible cameras deter theft. Active deterrent cameras with sirens and two-way audio let you confront thieves in real time from your phone. Pair with video intercom for secure delivery access.

Do you install intercoms and access control?

+

Yes. Video intercom, key fob entry, elevator access control, and smart doorbells throughout all service areas. We integrate intercom and camera systems for complete building security.

Is there a monthly monitoring fee?

+

No. Your NVR records locally with no cloud subscription. Remote smartphone access is free forever. No contracts, no recurring charges.

Do cameras work in dark basements and alleys?

+

Yes. Infrared night vision captures clear footage in complete darkness up to 120 feet. Full-color night vision models also available for critical areas.

How many cameras does a typical NYC property need?

+

Apartments need 2 to 4 cameras. Brownstones 4 to 8. Multi-unit buildings 8 to 32+. Suburban homes 6 to 10. We map placement during the free assessment.

Do you need a permit for security cameras in NYC?

+

Generally no. However, NYC landmark districts may require LPC approval for exterior hardware. We handle compliance for all installations.

Do you offer security camera repair?

+

Yes. Security camera repair, DVR/NVR troubleshooting, analog-to-digital upgrades, and system maintenance throughout all service areas.

What areas does Abstract Enterprises serve?

+

All five NYC boroughs — Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island — plus Long Island and the Hudson Valley.

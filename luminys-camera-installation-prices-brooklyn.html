How Luminys pricing works in Brooklyn

Each camera price includes the camera, mounting and a wire run up to 100 ft. The recorder price includes setup and the phone app. We always include enough hard drive for at least 2 weeks of continuous recording; longer recording is a bigger drive. Hidden in-wall wiring is $200 per camera.

Luminys installation questions

How much does Luminys camera installation cost in Brooklyn?

A complete 4-camera Luminys system installed in Brooklyn starts at $2,591, including the recorder, a hard drive with at least 2 weeks of recording, mounting, wiring up to 100 ft per camera and phone setup. Individual Luminys cameras run $465 to $4,215 each installed. Prices are plus sales tax.

What is included in the installed price?

The camera itself, mounting, a Cat6 run up to 100 ft per camera (exposed, attic or basement), termination and testing. The recorder price includes setup on your phone. We size the hard drive so you keep at least 2 weeks of recording.

Can you hide the wires?

Yes. Hidden in-wall wiring in hollow drywall is $200 more per camera, up to 100 ft. Spray foam, plaster, brick and exterior walls are checked on site.

Is this an exact quote?

It is an estimate so you know what to expect before we come out. Equipment prices change and every property is different, so your exact price comes after a quick look at the property.

What warranty do I get?

Equipment carries the manufacturer's parts warranty and every install includes our standard 30-day warranty. Our extended warranty, 1, 2 or 3 years, also covers the labor.

I already bought Luminys cameras. Will you install them?

Yes. We install customer-supplied cameras priced per line for your area. See our install-your-own pricing.

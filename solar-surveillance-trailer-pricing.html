Common Pricing Questions

Why does pricing change by area?
Travel distance, NYC vs HV/LI permit coordination, and site access complexity scale the price. Brooklyn is the baseline. Hudson Valley counties (Dutchess, Ulster) carry the highest surcharge because delivery is a 2+ hour drive each way plus potential overnight stays for multi-unit deployments.
Do I pay the full month upfront?
Yes — full monthly payment is charged at booking. This covers delivery, setup, one full 30-day term, and ongoing app access. After the first month, auto-renewal charges on the same day each month until you cancel 7 days before the next cycle.
What's the difference between "Rent" and "Buy Installed"?
Rent is a mobile towable trailer we deliver, park, and pick up when you're done. Good for projects with clear end dates (construction, events, temporary lots). Buy Installed is a permanent tower we physically install with concrete footing — designed to stay on your site for 5–10+ years. Best for permanent yards, dealer lots, industrial perimeters, or farm properties.
Can I finance the $30K purchase?
Yes. We structure custom financing around your project timeline. Typical terms: deposit at contract signing, installments tied to site survey / fabrication / shipment / install milestones, final payment at commissioning. No fixed templates — we tailor to how your project bills.
What happens if I need to extend past 30 days?
Month-to-month auto-renewal. Same rate, no re-setup fee. Long-term rentals (6 mo+) and annual pre-pay earn an pricing provided through a written AESS quote — ask before booking if you know you're going long.
Is Staten Island really +10%?
Yes. The Verrazano toll plus the extra drive time adds real cost. Same policy applies to every service silo we operate — it's baked into area pricing, not a surprise surcharge.
How do fleet discounts work?
Multi-unit pricing is provided through a written AESS quote. The discount is applied on your first invoice after we confirm fleet deployment. Multi-site fleets (e.g., one unit at each of three locations) don't typically qualify unless it's the same billing contract.
Can I get a COI with my rental?
Yes — included free. Standard commercial general liability coverage with your project listed as additional insured. Usually issued within 24 hours of booking. Required on most NYC construction sites anyway.

Name-brand camera systems, priced for your area.

Want a brand you know? We install Lorex, UniFi (Ubiquiti), Uniview and Luminys. Pick your area to see what a 4-camera system costs installed, then open any brand to price every model it makes, with the recorder, hard drive for at least 2 weeks of recording, mounting and phone app setup included.

What every package includes

Cameras mounted and wired (runs up to 100 ft per camera), the brand's recorder with a hard drive sized for at least 2 weeks of recording, setup on your phone, and a walkthrough before we leave. Prices are plus sales tax. Half down books your install date.

Options

Hidden in-wall wiring: +$200 per camera (hollow drywall, up to 100 ft). Spray foam, plaster, brick and exterior walls are checked on site. Longer recording: a bigger hard drive, quoted with your system. Monitor: view on your phone or TV, or add a monitor.

Warranty

Equipment is covered by the manufacturer's parts warranty, and every install includes our standard 30-day warranty. Our extended warranty covers the labor too, for 1, 2 or 3 years.

These are estimates

Prices include the equipment and installation in your area. Equipment prices change, and every property is different, so your exact quote comes after we see the site. Call or text (800) 486-0943 for a free estimate.

The Neighborhood

Why an Ulster County Camera Order Starts With the Road You Live On

Ask where a property sits in Ulster County and you already know half of what its camera system needs. Along the Thruway and Route 9W, from Saugerties through the City of Kingston and the Town of Ulster down to Esopus and Lloyd, the county is old brick and frame housing, apartments over shops, and the busiest retail strip between Albany and Newburgh. Follow Route 32 and Route 299 south into the Wallkill valley and you reach New Paltz, the SUNY campus, rental houses full of students, and orchard country in Marlborough, Plattekill and Gardiner under the Shawangunk ridge. Go west on Route 28 or Route 209 and you climb into the Catskill towns and the Rondout valley, where whole hamlets are weekend houses, cabins and short-term rentals, and where a phone can lose its signal for miles. Those are three different shopping lists, and we work out which one you are on before suggesting a camera.

The Census numbers draw the same map. The American Community Survey (2020–2024 five-year estimates) counts about 86,500 housing units across Ulster's twenty towns and one city. Countywide, 67.8 percent are single-family detached houses (table B25024), 30.1 percent of occupied homes are rented (table B25003), and 26.5 percent were built in 1939 or earlier (table B25034). Inside the City of Kingston the picture flips: only 43.2 percent of homes are detached, 51.6 percent are rented, and 56.8 percent are older than 1940. In Hurley, next door, 91.7 percent are detached houses. In Hardenburgh, at the far western edge of the county, 200 of the town's 288 homes are held for seasonal or occasional use.

An empty house is the textbook case for cameras, and in Ulster the empty houses are clustered exactly where the internet and cell service are weakest. That combination is the single biggest reason an Ulster camera order looks different from one in the river towns.

Where the thefts are reported

The New York State Division of Criminal Justice Services publishes index crimes by county and by police agency. For 2024 the Ulster County total was 1,469 property crimes, and 1,301 of them were larcenies; burglaries numbered 116 and motor vehicle thefts 52. Split by agency, the City of Kingston Police Department recorded 345 larcenies and 39 burglaries, and the Town of Ulster Police Department, which covers the Route 9W and Ulster Avenue shopping strip just north of the city line, recorded 240 larcenies. Together those two departments account for about 45 percent of the county's larcenies by our arithmetic. The State Police in Ulster recorded 233 larcenies and 22 burglaries, the New Paltz town and village department 100, Lloyd 96, the Sheriff's Office 94 and Saugerties 80. Woodstock recorded 12 larcenies and no burglaries at all.

Theft in Ulster is mostly theft of things where people shop and park. Break-ins are rarer and scattered across a hilly county, so a rural one can go unnoticed for days. The first problem needs a face at a register and a plate in a lot; the second needs a recorder that shows a vehicle came up a driveway that should have been empty.

A Town of Ulster store theft, start to finish

One case shows what store cameras are actually for. According to the Ulster County District Attorney's Office, on October 24, 2023 thieves took more than 100 items, including perfumes worth over $10,000 in total, from the Ulta Beauty store in the Town of Ulster. The store's video footage captured masked individuals inside. Town of Ulster police located three men after they fled in a car, News 12 reported at the time, and in March 2025 two members of the group were sentenced to up to three years in state prison after pleading guilty to grand larceny; the DA's office described a ring that targeted numerous retail stores in counties near Albany. District Attorney Emmanuel C. Nneji said: "This is scary and dangerous to the employees, law enforcement, and the public."

Masks defeat a single camera over the counter. What worked was the whole chain: video inside, a vehicle to follow, and police who could connect the two. In January 2026, Hudson Valley One reported another Town of Ulster arrest, of a man found in a neighboring town with the proceeds of a larceny from a business that had just happened: in and out, then onto the highway.

Car break-ins and a burglary in Esopus

The rural side of the county gets its own warnings. In January 2025, as reported by Hudson Valley One, the Ulster County Sheriff's Office warned residents around Union Center Road in the Town of Esopus after a residential burglary and several larcenies from vehicles over four days. The Sheriff's advice was practical: lock cars and houses, keep valuables out of sight, park in well-lit areas, and consider a security system and motion-sensor lights. A driveway camera with a vehicle-detection alert is the equipment that follows from that advice: it shows when it happened, which way they came and whether a vehicle was waiting.

Orchard country and farm buildings

Ulster is apple country, and the numbers are bigger than most people expect. The USDA's 2022 Census of Agriculture counted 486 farms in the county on 73,026 acres, an average of 150 acres each, with $105.5 million in products sold, up 94 percent from 2017. Apples were the county's top crop by acreage at 7,990 acres, ahead of hay and forage at 7,932. The county also had 1,693 horses and ponies. Orchards mean packing sheds, cold-storage rooms, farm markets with a register, pick-your-own parking fields, tractors and fuel parked overnight, and seasonal workers coming and going. A farm buying cameras is buying coverage of buildings spread across a property that may not share a single network cable.

Weather on the ridge: what a camera has to survive

The Mohonk Lake Cooperative Weather Station on the Shawangunk ridge above New Paltz has kept records since it was set up by the U.S. Weather Bureau in 1896, and the Mohonk Preserve publishes them. In 2023 the station recorded a low of −7°F on February 4, a high of 90°F, 65.86 inches of precipitation and 34.02 inches of snow. December 2024 brought a low of −1°F on the 22nd, 11 inches of snow, 5.01 inches of precipitation, and 16 days with measurable precipitation against an average of 13. That is a long run of wet days and freeze-thaw cycles, with a 97-degree swing across 2023. Winter security cameras and weatherproof security cameras for Ulster are picked from the spec sheet's operating range and housing rating, not from the word "outdoor" on the box, and the high ridges and Catskill hollows can run colder than the lake.

The dead zones west of Kingston

Cell coverage is the other thing that sets Ulster apart. In February 2020, Hudson Valley One reported that Shandaken, with twelve hamlets including Phoenicia, Pine Hill and Big Indian, was the only town on the Route 28 corridor where a cellphone call could not be made, with a 17-mile gap in Verizon service between Middletown in Delaware County and the Town of Olive. The Town Board passed a resolution asking for service, and Town Clerk Joyce Grant told the paper: "This is not quaint anymore." Coverage may have changed since; either way, a camera system for the Catskill towns has to record on site without needing a signal, and any remote viewing has to ride on whatever wired or fixed-wireless internet the house has. That rules out a large share of the battery cameras sold online, which upload to the cloud and do little without a connection.

Kingston's historic districts

Kingston adds one more rule. The City of Kingston's code (chapter 405) requires a certificate of appropriateness from the Historic Landmarks Preservation Commission for "any material change in the appearance of such a property, its light fixtures, signs, sidewalks, fences, steps, paving or other exterior elements visible from a public street or alley" in its landmark districts, which include the Stockade, Rondout-West Strand and Chestnut Street districts. A camera on a front facade in Uptown is an exterior element visible from the street. That does not mean no cameras; it means small, neutral-colored housings placed under cornices, in doorways and on rear walls, and checking with the city before drilling a street-facing wall. We sell compact turrets and mini domes in white and black for exactly this reason.

Outdoor security cameras for wet, windy Ulster walls

Outdoor surveillance cameras in this county deal with more water than cold. Mohonk Lake logged 65.86 inches of precipitation in 2023, and December 2024 had 16 days of measurable rain or snow. Water finds its way into a camera through the cable connection behind it far more often than through the housing, which is why we pair every outdoor camera with a junction box or a sealed mount and check the housing rating on the spec sheet. Weatherproof security cameras rated IP66 or IP67 keep driven rain and blowing snow out; the house-brand F, E and D4/D5 kits list IP67 cameras, and the C series and the D1 to D3 4K kits list IP66. On the ridges, a firm mount on a solid surface keeps a camera aimed where you left it in the wind.

Indoor security cameras

Indoor surveillance cameras cover what happens after someone is through the door: the register in an Uptown Kingston gift shop, the stockroom behind a Main Street storefront in New Paltz, the stairs of a Midtown two-family, a packing room in Marlboro, the office of a Saugerties marina. PoE, WiFi or coax

PoE security cameras take power and video over one network cable from the recorder or a switch, so there is no outlet to find on an outside wall and no battery to swap in a cold snap. A single Ethernet run covers about 328 feet (100 meters); beyond that, a switch, an extender, fiber or a wireless bridge carries it on. WiFi security cameras need a strong signal and a nearby outlet, and stone walls and steel farm siding weaken it. Coax cameras on a DVR still make sense where an older system left coax runs that test clean, a common situation in commercial buildings along Broadway and Ulster Avenue. Do PoE security cameras need internet? No. They record to the NVR on the property whether or not the internet is up, which is the whole point in a town with weak service. The internet only carries alerts and remote viewing.

Night vision on roads with no streetlights

Outside Kingston, New Paltz and the village centers, Ulster nights are genuinely dark. Infrared security cameras light the scene with invisible IR and record in black and white; on the house-brand spec sheets that reach runs from 60 feet (A1) to 120 feet (the C1, C3, D1 and D3 eyeballs). Color night vision cameras and full color night vision cameras add a soft white light or a very sensitive sensor so the recording keeps colors, the difference between “a truck” and “a red truck.” Starlight security cameras and low light security cameras work best with a little ambient light; on a pitch-dark cabin drive we add a floodlight or a spotlight model.

Smart detection for bears, deer and headlights

A camera that only senses motion will message you for headlights sweeping across from Route 28, a downpour, swaying branches, deer and the occasional black bear. AI security cameras that classify people and vehicles keep the recording complete but send the phone alert only when a person reaches a door or a car enters the drive. Smart motion detection cameras, object detection security cameras and video analytics security cameras vary by model; we call a camera “AI” only when its spec sheet does. On the house-brand side, the D4 and D5 kits list human and vehicle detection; the Reolink Duo 3 PoE adds animal detection, useful in bear country. License plate recognition cameras, LPR cameras, ANPR cameras and facial recognition security cameras are a separate category, and we use those names only for models whose maker does.

Surge protection and power for summer storms and long outages

Outdoor cable runs on hilltop houses and long runs to a barn or a gate can carry a surge from a nearby lightning strike back to the recorder. We sell inline surge protectors for network and coax runs, grounded at the building where the cable enters, so a strike near a camera is less likely to take out the recorder and every other camera with it. For outages, a battery backup (UPS) sized to the recorder and the PoE switch keeps the system recording for a while after the power drops.

Network video recorders (NVR)

IP cameras record to a network video recorder. When the NVR has PoE ports, each camera plugs straight into the back of it for power and video, which is the neat way to do four or eight cameras; larger jobs hang a separate PoE switch off it. Picking one is a matter of three specs: how many channels, the top camera resolution it decodes, and how many drives it holds.

Digital video recorders (DVR)

Coax cameras record to a digital video recorder. Kingston storefronts, Saugerties offices and older Ellenville buildings often still have coax from an earlier system, and if it tests clean an HD DVR puts new cameras on it without pulling cable. Our A, F and C kits are DVR-based, from 1080P to 4K. If the old coax is cracked, wet, or stapled to a wall that is about to be re-sided, moving to IP is usually the better buy.

Surveillance hard drives

Desktop drives are not made for round-the-clock writing; CCTV hard drives are. We carry 4TB surveillance hard drives, 8TB surveillance hard drives and 16TB surveillance storage on one or several drives, sized to how many days you want to keep. Buy a recorder with a spare bay and you can add capacity later, for instance when a host adds a fifth camera to the deck approach after the first season.

PoE switches, injectors, surge protectors and power

When the recorder's ports run out, or when a garage, barn or packing shed needs several cameras, a PoE switch out in that building lets them all share one cable home. A security camera PoE injector covers a single camera at the end of a long run. CCTV power supplies and CCTV power adapters feed coax cameras and anything not on PoE. Spotlight, siren and heater models pull extra watts on a cold night, so the switch's power budget is sized to the actual cameras, and any run that crosses between buildings gets surge protection.

Linking the shed, the barn and the gate

  • Up to roughly 328 feet: a single outdoor or direct-burial Cat6 cable to the recorder, with a surge protector where it enters the building.
  • Somewhat past that: an extender in the middle of the run, or a compact PoE switch inside the outbuilding fed from the main building.
  • Across a farm or a campground: fiber between buildings, with a media converter at each end; fiber does not carry lightning surges between buildings.
  • Ledge rock, a road, or no way to dig: a pair of point-to-point wireless radios in line of sight, mounted on the outside of any steel wall.
  • No power at the gate: a solar kit with a battery feeding the camera and the bridge, sized to December daylight.

How to prevent security camera hacking

  • Replace every factory password and issue separate logins for the owner, the manager, the caretaker and the cleaner.

Security camera laws for Ulster homes and businesses

Are security cameras legal in Ulster County? Recording video on property you own or control is legal; New York law draws a few hard lines around it. General information only, not legal advice:

  • Audio. New York Penal Law §250.05 treats eavesdropping, which includes mechanically overhearing a conversation without the consent of at least one party, as a class E felony. So the microphones on audio recording security cameras and two way audio security cameras stay switched off in shops, offices, rentals and shared spaces unless there is a clear purpose and clear notice.
  • Private places. Recording people undressing, or in places where they reasonably expect privacy such as bathrooms, bedrooms and changing areas, is a crime under Penal Law §250.45.
  • Employees. Employers may not video record employees in restrooms, locker rooms or rooms set aside for changing clothes, except under a court order, according to Labor Law §203-c.
  • Rentals. Landlords can cover common areas they control; a tenant's unit is off limits. Short-term rental platforms add their own rules, and Airbnb bans indoor cameras entirely.
  • Historic districts. In Kingston's landmark districts, visible exterior changes need a certificate of appropriateness under city code chapter 405.
  • Signs. New York City's biometric signage rule lives in the city's own code and stops at the city line, so it does not reach Ulster. A posted sign still discourages trouble for very little money.

Beyond statutes, CCTV regulations and security camera privacy laws can arrive through a lease, HOA bylaws, a business license or town zoning, so read those before putting a camera on a shared or street-facing wall.

Buying Security Cameras in Ulster County: Questions Answered

Do I need the Kingston landmarks commission's sign-off before a camera goes on a Stockade or Rondout building?

If the camera changes the look of an exterior element visible from a street or alley in a landmark district, the city code says a certificate of appropriateness is required, in addition to any building permit. Call the city's preservation office before drilling a street-facing wall. Cameras inside, in a recessed doorway or on a rear wall out of public view usually avoid the question, and small neutral turrets are the easiest case to make if a facade camera is needed.

Which camera parts survive a lightning storm on a Shawangunk ridge house better?

No camera survives a direct strike. What helps is limiting the damage from a nearby one: inline surge protectors on every outdoor run, bonded to a proper ground where the cable enters the house, a surge-protected power strip or UPS for the recorder and switch, and fiber instead of copper between separate buildings. Then a strike might cost one camera instead of the whole system.

What should a Marlboro or Highland orchard watch at the farm market and the cold-storage doors?

The market register and the door customers use, the pick-your-own entrance and the parking field, each cold-storage and packing-shed door, and the fuel tank and equipment area. A 4K camera on the parking field shows vehicles and people at once. Cold-storage doors get cameras outside the cold room, aimed at the door, because condensation on a lens moving in and out of a cooler blurs the picture.

How much drive space does a licensed cannabis shop in Ulster need to keep 60 days of video?

Work it out from the camera count. At about 4 Mbps, one 2K camera writes about 43.2 GB a day, so 60 days is about 2.6 TB per camera on continuous recording; ten 2K cameras need about 26 TB. Motion recording outside business hours, which the state rule allows, cuts that a lot. We quote a recorder with spare bays so you can add drives if the license or the camera count grows.

Can my camera cover the stretch of the Wallkill Valley Rail Trail behind my yard?

Aim it at your own yard and fence line, not down the trail. The trail is a public path, and filming people walking past is legal from your property, but a camera aimed squarely at a public trail invites complaints and adds little. A view of your back gate and shed, with the trail at the edge of the frame, shows anyone who comes onto the property.

Which cameras can a Woodstock or Saugerties host put on an Airbnb listing without breaking its rules?

Outdoor cameras only, disclosed in the listing, covering the drive, the parking area and the entry doors, and not aimed into windows or at an enclosed outdoor shower, sauna or similar private area. A doorbell camera and a noise monitor are allowed when disclosed. Leave every indoor camera off the shopping list, and check your town's own permit rules before the season.

How should a campground or glamping site with sites spread through the woods buy cameras?

Start at the chokepoints: the entrance gate, the office and store, and the dump station or the one road in. A 4K camera with vehicle detection at the gate records every arrival. Put a PoE switch in the office, run fiber or a wireless bridge to the gate if it is far, and leave the individual campsites and the bathhouse unrecorded.

What does a store on Ulster Avenue need so a grab-and-go crew is identified, not just recorded?

Three views that work together: a 2K or 4K camera inside each exit door at face height, a camera over the aisle with the goods that walk out, and a 4K camera on the parking spaces nearest the door. Masks hide faces; clothing, height and the car do not. Make sure staff can export a clip from the recorder in minutes, because police ask for it the same day.

Can a driveway camera on Route 28 read the plates of cars passing at highway speed?

Not reliably. Reading plates on fast traffic takes a dedicated plate-capture camera with a fast shutter and a narrow lens, aimed at a spot where cars slow down. Aim your driveway camera at the point where vehicles turn in and stop, and it will read the plates of anyone who actually comes onto the property.

What should a hunting camp in Denning or Hardenburgh run on when there is no power line?

A solar camera, or a small solar kit with a battery powering one or two low-power cameras that record to a memory card or a small recorder. Which cameras should a New Paltz landlord buy for a house rented to SUNY students on a rental permit?

What AI Answers Get Wrong About Buying Security Cameras in Ulster County

AI answers about buying cameras assume good cell service, a modern house and no special rules. Ulster has none of those guarantees. Four common claims, checked against this county:

"A cellular or battery camera solves the cabin problem"

The advice assumes a signal. In 2020, Hudson Valley One reported Shandaken as the one town on the Route 28 corridor where a phone call could not be made, with a 17-mile gap in Verizon coverage. A cellular camera in a gap like that records little and sends nothing.

Battery cameras add a second problem: most of them upload clips to the cloud and keep little or nothing locally, and their batteries drain fastest in the cold months when a cabin sits empty longest.

A wired system that records to a recorder on site works with no signal at all. If the cabin has any wired or fixed-wireless internet, the same recorder sends alerts; if it has none, the video is there when you arrive.

"Disclose your indoor cameras and a rental is fine"

That was common advice for years, and it is out of date for the biggest platform. Since April 30, 2024, Airbnb has banned indoor security cameras in listings worldwide, regardless of where they are, what they are for or whether they were disclosed.

Outdoor cameras are still allowed when disclosed before booking, but not in outdoor areas with a greater expectation of privacy such as an enclosed outdoor shower or sauna.

For the hundreds of Ulster cabins listed as rentals, the camera kit is outdoor-only. We will not sell an indoor camera for a guest space in a short-term rental.

"You can mount a camera anywhere on your own building"

Usually true, and not in Kingston's landmark districts. City code chapter 405 requires a certificate of appropriateness for material changes to exterior elements visible from a public street or alley in the Stockade, Rondout-West Strand and Chestnut Street districts.

That does not ban cameras. It means checking before drilling a street-facing wall, and choosing small, neutral housings placed under cornices, in recessed doorways and on rear walls.

The same thinking applies to a lease, an HOA, or a license condition: the building may be yours, but someone else may have a say in what goes on the outside of it.

"Store cameras belong over the register"

The register camera matters, but the Ulta case in the Town of Ulster shows its limits. The thieves wore masks inside the store; the case advanced because police could follow a vehicle.

A store camera plan in this county starts with the exit doors from inside at face height, adds the aisles with high-value goods, and finishes with a 4K view of the parking spaces nearest the entrance.

Register cameras then confirm the transaction. Outside-only or register-only systems record part of the story; the three views together record all of it.

FAQ

Ulster County Security Cameras FAQ

Can I order cameras for a Catskill cabin I only reach on weekends and have them waiting there?
+

Yes, if someone can receive them. Tell us who will be at the cabin, or which neighbor or caretaker holds a key, and we arrange delivery to that address when the order is confirmed.

Will freezing rain coat a dome camera's bubble on an Ulster County house?
+

It can. A dome's clear bubble collects a glaze of ice in freezing rain, and the infrared light then bounces off the ice and whites out the night picture. Turret and bullet cameras keep the lens behind a short hood or a flat window, so they clear faster. Under-eave mounting helps any shape, and we save domes for spots inside or under a roof.

Can a tenant in an apartment over an Uptown Kingston shop put a camera on the shared stairs?
+

Not without the landlord's permission, because the stairs are a common area the owner controls and the lease usually covers changes to it. A tenant can put a camera inside their own apartment, aimed at their own door, with the microphone off. The cleaner answer is for the building owner to buy one camera for the stairs and share a view-only login with each tenant.

Do the cameras you sell keep working in a Shandaken hollow with no cell signal?
+

Recording does, because a wired system writes to its own hard drive on site and does not need a signal at all. What a dead zone takes away is the alert to your phone and live viewing from away. If the property has any wired or fixed-wireless internet, the recorder uses that. If it has none, the system still records, and you review it when you arrive.

Can cameras watch my car at a Thruway park-and-ride in New Paltz or Kingston?
+

No. Park-and-ride lots belong to the state or the operator, and you cannot mount your own camera there. A dash camera in the car, recording while parked if it supports that, is the private-owner option. Cameras we sell go on property you own or control, such as your driveway or a business lot.

Can a camera help me prove when a contractor arrived at my Ulster County house?
+

Yes. A camera on the driveway records the time a vehicle arrives and leaves, and the recorder stamps every clip with the date and time. Keep the recorder's clock synced to the internet time server so the stamps are right. A plain notice that the driveway is recorded keeps the arrangement open with everyone who works on the house.

Does a church or meeting house in Ulster County need anything special from a camera system?
+

Mostly restraint. Cover the doors, the parking area, the office and any room where offerings are counted, and leave cameras out of restrooms, changing rooms and counseling rooms. Keep microphones off during services and meetings. Give the people who unlock the building their own logins so the recordings are not shared on one password.

Can I put cameras up for the winter at a summer bungalow colony and take them down in spring?
+

You can, but leaving them up is usually better. A closed colony is most exposed from November to April, and cameras mounted under eaves on junction boxes ride out the winter. If you do want them removed for the season, we can use mounts that leave the junction box behind so the cameras go back up in the same spots.

Will bears knocking over the trash show up as people on smart detection?
+

Sometimes. A bear standing upright at a trash shed can look enough like a person to trigger a person alert on some cameras. Cameras that list animal detection separately handle it better, and drawing the person-detection zone around the doors rather than the trash area cuts most false alerts. The recorder still keeps the whole event.

Can one recorder cover both my New Paltz store and the apartment upstairs?
+

Yes, if you own the building and keep the views separate. One recorder can hold the store cameras and the cameras on the apartment entrance and stairs, with separate logins so the shop staff see only the store and the tenant sees only the entry. Nothing ever points into the apartment itself.

Do you sell cameras that will run off a solar panel at an Ulster field or gate?
+

Yes. For a gate or a field edge with no power, we supply a solar camera, or a solar kit with a battery that powers a PoE camera and a wireless link. Short December days set the size of the panel and the battery. For seasonal coverage of a whole field or a construction site, a solar surveillance trailer is often the simpler buy.

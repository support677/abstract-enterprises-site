1. What Angi and HomeAdvisor quote vs. what a Uniview install costs in Rockland County

2. Why the “is Uniview cheap?” framing misleads

The internet often files Uniview under “budget brand.” That undersells it. Uniview (UNV) is a top global professional IP-camera maker, a full ONVIF member, with ColorHunter and LightHunter low-light technology that competes with far pricier lines.

ColorHunter delivers full color down to about 0.0003 Lux with a large F1.0 aperture, a BSI sensor, and warm-white LEDs; LightHunter holds color to roughly 0.002 Lux. On dark Pomona estate grounds, a wooded Suffern driveway, or an unlit apartment garage in Spring Valley, that is the difference between a usable face or plate and a gray smear.

So “cheaper” is real on the invoice but misleading on the spec sheet — professional-grade hardware at a mid-market price, which is exactly why we install it across Rockland estates, apartment buildings, suburban homes, and historic river-town properties.

For a private Rockland homeowner, board, or business — an estate, an apartment building, a shop — Uniview is legal to buy, install, and operate. The restriction targets who is buying (federal agencies, critical infrastructure), not a blanket consumer ban.

Plain-English Glossary

Uniview Terminology, Explained

Uniview Prime

Uniview’s full-color night vision — a wide aperture and warm supplement light capture color 24/7 instead of black-and-white infrared.

Uniview AI

On-camera AI that classifies people and vehicles versus other motion, cutting false alarms and labeling footage so it’s searchable.

NVR

Network Video Recorder — records all cameras, powers them over PoE, and serves footage to your phone. The heart of the system.

PoE

Power over Ethernet — one cable carries power and data to each camera, ideal for estate runs and multi-family buildings alike.

IP66 / IP67

Weatherproof ratings — dust-tight and protected against heavy rain and snow, essential for exterior estate cameras.

PTZ

Pan-Tilt-Zoom — a motorized dome that moves and zooms to cover a long driveway, estate grounds, or a parking lot from one point.

Vandal-Resistant (IK)

An impact rating — IK10 housings resist tampering, useful for accessible apartment-building lobby and garage cameras.

the Uniview NVR app

The app for live view, playback, and alerts on your phone — multi-user for managers and owners, no monthly fee for local recording.

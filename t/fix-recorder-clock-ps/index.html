Fix your camera clock

Daylight saving ended, so the recorder is an hour off. Watch this short video, then do the 5 steps at the recorder with the monitor and mouse.

  1. Right-click the screen and click Main Menu (log in if it asks).
  2. Go to System › General › Date & Time.
  3. Time Zone: GMT-05:00 (New York).
  4. Check DST, Type Week: starts 2nd Sunday of March, ends 1st Sunday of November.
  5. Check NTP so the time stays correct by itself. Then click Apply and Save.

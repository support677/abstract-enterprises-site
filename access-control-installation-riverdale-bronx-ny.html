The Riverdale Historic District was designated by the New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission on October 16, 1990 — making Riverdale the city's 54th historic district. Buildings within the District require LPC review for any modification visible from a public street, including exterior reader hardware, exterior cable conduit, lobby panel placement visible through entry windows, etc. Standard LPC playbook for District buildings: concealed Cat6 cable runs entirely through existing interior conduit, through-bolt strikes inside the door frame, reader placement on inside vestibule wall ONLY (never on exterior masonry / carved stone trim / original wood paneling), paint-matched flush-mount only when interior placement is impossible, period-appropriate brass / oil-rubbed bronze hardware finish, removal-rather-than-replacement of original entry hardware whenever historically significant.

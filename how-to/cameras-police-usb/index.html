CAMERA HOW-TO

Copy video to a USB drive

Right from your recorder — the full copy police and insurance usually want. Quick clip instead? Save it on your phone ›

  1. Plug a USB drive into the recorder. When USB device found pops up, click File Backup. No pop-up? Right-click › Main Menu › Backup. (Not Config Backup — that's settings.)
  2. Pick the camera, set Type to All, and enter the start and end time — a few minutes before and after. Click Search.
  3. Check every file in the list that covers what happened.
  4. Choose MP4 (plays on any computer) and click Backup. Wait for Backup successful before pulling the drive.
  5. Open it on a computer to confirm the right camera and time. Keep the original; give them a copy.

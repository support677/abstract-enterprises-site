CAMERA HOW-TO

Playback on your recorder

Right on the monitor, with the mouse. On your phone instead? Watch phone playback ›

  1. Right-click anywhere on the screen and choose Playback (some recorders call it Search). Log in with your recorder password if asked.
  2. On the right side, pick the day on the calendar and check the camera(s) you want.
  3. Click the colored part of the time bar at the bottom. Green = regular recording, yellow = motion. Playback starts where you click.
  4. Use the zoom buttons (24h › 2h › 1h › 30m) to narrow it to the hour, and fast forward to move quickly.
  5. Right-click to back out to live view.

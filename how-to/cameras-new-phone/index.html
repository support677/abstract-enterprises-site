CAMERA HOW-TO

New phone? Get your cameras back

About 2 minutes in the DMSS app.

  1. Download the free DMSS app (App Store or Google Play).
  2. Log in with the same account you used before. Your system comes right back.
  3. No account? Tap + › Scan QR code.
  4. The QR code is on the sticker on your recorder, or on your monitor: Main Menu › Network › P2P.
  5. Username is usually admin. Enter your recorder password and save.

Stuck or don't know the password? Call or text (800) 486-0943.

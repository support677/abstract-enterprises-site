CAMERA HOW-TO

Save a clip for police

From your phone, in the DMSS app. Need a full copy? Copy it to a USB drive ›

  1. Open Playback, pick the camera and the day, and drag the timeline to just before it happened.
  2. Tap the scissors to start the clip, let it play through, tap stop, then tap OK.
  3. Keep the app open while it saves (a few seconds).
  4. Go to Home › Me › My Files › Video. Your clip is there.
  5. Tap Edit, select the clip, tap Save. It's now in your phone's photos, ready to text or email.

Tips: start a few minutes before and end a few minutes after. Don't wait — old video gets recorded over.

CAMERA HOW-TO

Got an issue? Start here

Where your warranty, the app's built-in help and your recorder's info live — so you can fix most things fast.

  1. Your warranty is on the invoice we sent you by email or text. Open it and scroll to the bottom. Save that email.
  2. Help inside the DMSS app: tap Me › Help and Feedback for answers to the most common problems.
  3. Camera won't play? Tap Help right on that video box.
  4. Recorder info: right-click › Main Menu › Info › Version for your model and serial number (also on the sticker under the recorder).
  5. Is it recording? Main Menu › Storage › Disk Manager. Status "Normal" means you're good. A full drive is normal — it records over the oldest video.

Still stuck? Text us your model and serial number — Call or text (800) 486-0943.

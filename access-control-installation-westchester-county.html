Westchester’s access control needs are driven by three distinct property profiles. Second, southern Westchester cities — Yonkers (the state’s fourth-largest city), Mount Vernon, and New Rochelle — have dense apartment building stock similar to the Bronx, where lobby fob systems, buzzer upgrades, and elevator floor restriction address the same unauthorized entry and package theft problems found in NYC. Property crime in Westchester costs $975 per household annually. The county’s property crime rate is concentrated in its cities, while burglary and vehicle break-ins spike seasonally in suburban residential communities during holiday periods and summer vacation months when homes are unoccupied.

Legacy 125kHz Fob Cloning in Westchester Apartment & Condo Buildings

What is the difference between an RFID transponder and a gate remote for a Westchester home?+

A gate remote is a handheld clicker using infrared or RF signals that can be copied and has no audit trail. An RFID transponder mounts behind your windshield and is detected automatically by a long-range reader at 15 to 30 feet. Transponders use encrypted signals, log every entry, and open the gate hands-free as you approach. No button pressing, no window rolling down.

In most cases, yes. We work with gate motor installers to motorize existing swing and slide gates, then add the access control layer. If the gate structure cannot support motorization, we can recommend fabricators who build custom gates designed for motor and access integration.

Can I see who is at my Westchester gate when I am not home?+

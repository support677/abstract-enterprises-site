Security Alarm Installer Work: What the Job Covers

Alarm panels and keypads

2GIG, Qolsys, DSC and Honeywell panels, keypads and touchscreens, set up and tied to the app.

Sensors

Door and window contacts, motion detectors, glass-break sensors and smoke detectors on the alarm.

Monitoring and the app

Making sure every zone reports to the central station and walking the customer through the app before you leave.

Service calls

Troubleshooting false alarms, dead sensors, low batteries and panels that lost communication.

Brands you'll work on: 2GIG, Qolsys, DSC, Honeywell, Alarm.com.

Career Questions

Alarm Technician Careers in NYC

What does an alarm technician do?
+

An alarm technician installs and services intrusion alarm systems: the panel, keypads, door and window contacts, motion detectors and the monitoring connection. Service calls cover false alarms, dead sensors and communication failures.

What's on a security alarm installer job description?
+

Install alarm panels and sensors, program zones, connect the system to central station monitoring, set up the customer's app, and troubleshoot alarms on service calls. A license, your own vehicle and your own hand tools are required here.

Is a security system installer the same as an alarm installer?
+

Mostly. Security system installers often handle cameras and access control as well as alarms. We hire for alarms specifically in this posting, but techs who can do all three earn more.

Can I be an alarm installer with no experience?
+

Yes, as a helper at the bottom of the range. You learn panel installs and sensor placement on real jobs. Experienced installers start at the top.

FAQ

Security Alarm Installer Jobs in New York City: Questions

What alarm panels do your installers work on?
+

Most new systems are 2GIG. On service calls you'll see DSC, Honeywell/Resideo, Qolsys and older panels, so you need to be able to program more than one brand.

Do alarm installers set up monitoring?
+

Yes. You connect the system to central station monitoring and set up the customer's app, then test it with them before you leave.

How much do security alarm installers make in NYC?
+

We pay $20–$36 an hour: experienced installers $28 and up, helpers with less experience from $20. The Bureau of Labor Statistics put the average for security and fire alarm installers in the New York metro at $27.82 an hour in May 2022.

Can you install security cameras on a Brooklyn brownstone without damaging the brick?

Yes. Our licensed technicians specialize in brownstone installations throughout Brooklyn. We use masonry-specific anchoring designed for brick and limestone facades. Cables are routed through interior walls, basements, and existing conduit — not strapped across the front of your building. When exterior cable runs are unavoidable, we use color-matched covers that blend with the facade. We work in designated historic districts including Fort Greene, Cobble Hill, Brooklyn Heights, and Park Slope without damaging or altering protected architecture.

Are wired or wireless security cameras better for Brooklyn apartments and buildings?

Wired PoE (Power-over-Ethernet) cameras are significantly more reliable in Brooklyn. The borough's construction is dominated by brick, concrete, and plaster walls that block WiFi signals between floors and rooms. Wireless cameras frequently lose connection, drop frames, and fail when you need them most. Hardwired PoE cameras receive both power and data through a single Ethernet cable, delivering stable 4K video 24/7 regardless of wall thickness or WiFi congestion. If reliability matters, go wired.

How do I stop package theft from my Brooklyn stoop or apartment lobby?

Package theft is the number one reason Brooklyn homeowners call us. A visible security camera at your front entrance is a proven crime deterrent — most package thieves move on when they see a camera. For active protection, our active deterrent cameras trigger a built-in siren, strobe light, and two-way audio when motion is detected, letting you confront the thief from your phone in real time. Pair it with a video doorbell for complete stoop and vestibule coverage. We install these systems on brownstones, walk-ups, and apartment building lobbies across Brooklyn every week.

Do I need a permit to install security cameras in Brooklyn, New York?

In most cases, no permit is required for standard residential or commercial security camera installation in Brooklyn. However, there are rules: you cannot record audio without consent in New York State (two-party consent law), and cameras cannot be pointed into a neighbor's private space. In NYC landmark districts, exterior mounting may require Landmarks Preservation Commission approval for visible hardware changes. Our technicians understand all local regulations and install code-compliant systems throughout Brooklyn.

A standard brownstone or townhouse usually needs 4 to 6 cameras. Multi-unit buildings need more — typically 8 to 16 covering lobbies, hallways, stairwells, and the building perimeter. What's the difference between IP cameras and analog CCTV cameras?

IP cameras connect via Ethernet cable (Cat5e/Cat6) to an NVR and deliver HD or 4K resolution with smart features like human/vehicle detection, two-way audio, and remote access. Analog CCTV cameras use coaxial cable and connect to a DVR — they're lower resolution but still effective and cost-efficient, especially if your building already has coax wiring in place. For new installations, we recommend IP cameras. For upgrades to existing coax systems, HD analog cameras are a smart budget option. We install both.

Can security cameras record in the dark in Brooklyn alleys and backyards?

Yes. Every outdoor camera we install has infrared night vision that captures clear black-and-white footage in complete darkness — up to 100 to 120 feet depending on the model. Our premium models also include full-color night vision using a built-in warm LED light, so you get color footage even at night. This is especially important for Brooklyn rear yards, side alleys, basement entrances, and poorly lit parking areas where most property crime occurs after dark.

Brooklyn Security Camera Questions Answered

How much does security camera installation cost in Brooklyn, NY?

+

Residential security camera installation in Brooklyn typically ranges from $850 to $2,500 for a 4- to 8-camera security camera system with an NVR and professional wiring. Can you install cameras on brownstones without damaging the facade?

+

Absolutely. Our licensed technicians specialize in brownstone security camera installation across all Brooklyn neighborhoods. We use masonry-specific anchoring, route cables through interior walls and basements, and use color-matched cable covers for exterior runs. We preserve historic architecture in designated landmark districts throughout Brooklyn.

Are wired PoE cameras or wireless better for Brooklyn buildings?

+

For Brooklyn's brick-and-concrete construction, wired PoE (Power-over-Ethernet) cameras are far more reliable. Thick masonry walls interfere with WiFi signals, making wireless security cameras unreliable across multiple floors. Hardwired PoE systems deliver stable power and consistent HD video transmission even through pre-war construction.

Can I view my security cameras remotely from my phone?

+

Yes — every video surveillance system we install includes remote video monitoring via smartphone, tablet, or desktop. Pull up live camera feeds, review recorded footage, and receive motion detection alerts from anywhere. Our systems support 24/7 surveillance with live streaming directly to your device.

Can security cameras help prevent package theft in Brooklyn?

+

It's one of the top reasons Brooklyn property owners call us. A visible surveillance camera is a proven crime deterrent. Our active deterrent cameras go further — triggering built-in sirens, strobe lights, and two-way audio for crime prevention in real time from your phone. Combined with a video doorbell, it's the most effective solution.

How many cameras do I need for my Brooklyn property?

+

Most Brooklyn residential properties need 4 to 8 cameras. Key positions include front entrance, rear yard, hallways, side passages, and parking areas. We map it all during the free on-site security assessment and provide a free quote with exact camera placement recommendations.

Do I need WiFi for the NVR to record?

+

No. Our NVR systems record locally to an on-site hard drive — no internet required for recording or 24/7 surveillance. You only need WiFi for remote access and push notifications. No cloud storage fees and no monthly monitoring charges.

What's the difference between a DVR and NVR?

+

DVR works with analog CCTV cameras over coaxial cable. NVR works with HD IP cameras over Ethernet (Cat5e/Cat6), delivering higher resolution and easier remote access. For new installations in Brooklyn, we recommend NVR systems for best quality and reliability.

“The handyman I hired cracked my fieldstone trying to mount the TV”

Fieldstone is irregular and brittle at the edges. You need carbide-tipped masonry bits, low-speed drilling, and anchors placed in the thickest part of each stone — not at edges or in mortar joints. Will mounting a TV damage my stone fireplace?

Professional installation creates small, clean holes in stone that are invisible behind the mount. We use carbide-tipped masonry bits at low speed to prevent cracking. Pilot holes confirm stone depth before committing to full-size anchors.

Remsen Village-Specific TV Installation Challenges

🧱 Mixed Plaster & Drywall in Renovated Rowhouses

Problem: Many Remsen Village rowhouses have been partially renovated — original plaster on some walls, new drywall on others, and sometimes both within the same room. Using the wrong anchor type for the wrong wall means the TV doesn’t hold.

Solution: We test every planned mounting surface before drilling — knock test, magnet test, pilot drill. Toggle bolts for plaster, standard anchors for drywall on wood studs. We switch hardware mid-installation as wall types change.

🚇 2/5 Subway Vibration Along Nostrand

Problem: The IRT Nostrand Avenue Line (2/5 trains) runs through western Remsen Village with stations at Church Avenue, Beverly Road, and Newkirk Avenue–Little Haiti. Buildings near these stations experience micro-vibration.

Solution: Lock washers, thread-lock compound, vibration-dampening rubber washers. Fixed mounts recommended near subway corridors.

🔥 Basement Recreation Rooms

Problem: Remsen Village homeowners frequently want TVs in finished basements and recreation rooms. Basement walls are often poured concrete or cinder block — impossible to drill with standard equipment. Humidity and moisture can also affect long-term mount integrity.

Solution: Hammer drill with masonry bits for concrete/block walls. Tapcon or sleeve anchors. Stainless steel mounting hardware resists corrosion in below-grade humidity. Surface raceways for cable management on concrete.

🏠 Multi-Room Homeowner Setups

Problem: Remsen Village’s high homeownership rate means many customers want TVs in 3+ rooms — living room, master bedroom, basement, and sometimes a rental unit upstairs. Coordinating multiple wall types, mount types, and device connections across a whole house takes planning.

Solution: Single-visit multi-room installations at 10% off for 2+ TVs. We assess all rooms, bring hardware for every wall type, and complete the entire house in one appointment. Cat6 cable runs between floors for shared media distribution.

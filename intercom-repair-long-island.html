Long Island Intercom Repair Terminology

  • Gate station / pedestal — Intercom panel at the bottom of the driveway, mounted on a post.
  • Telephone entry — Older gate systems that dial a resident's phone when a visitor presses their code.
  • Cellular gate intercom — Modern replacement using LTE instead of landline.
  • Marine-grade / IP66 panel — Outdoor intercom rated for salt-air and direct weather exposure.
  • Conformal coating — Protective spray on circuit boards to resist salt corrosion.
  • Master / sub-station — Residential intercoms with main panel + remote handsets.
  • SIP / cloud intercom — Internet-based intercoms routing calls to smartphones.
  • Transformer / power supply — Steps down to intercom voltage. Storm-vulnerable.
  • Bus / trunk wiring — Shared cable connecting all stations.

The Work in Brooklyn

What Security Camera Installer Work Looks Like in Brooklyn

Brooklyn camera jobs are row houses, brownstones, small apartment buildings and storefronts. The hard part is running cable on brick and through old buildings without leaving it ugly. On a brownstone, you plan a route from the roof, rear yard or basement so the cable disappears.

  • Brownstone fronts and rear yards
  • Apartment building hallways, lobbies and entrances
  • Delis and storefronts
  • Cable routes on brick that don't show

Questions

Security Camera Installer Jobs in Brooklyn: Common Questions

How do you run camera cable on Brooklyn brick?

You plan the route first: along the cornice, down a leader line or through the basement, so the cable is hard to see. Drilling brick cleanly is part of the job.

Do Brooklyn camera jobs include apartment buildings?

Yes. Buildings want cameras at entrances, lobbies, hallways and mailrooms, with one recorder for the whole building.

What do Brooklyn delis usually want?

Cameras on the register, the door and the aisles, and the owner's phone set up to watch from home.

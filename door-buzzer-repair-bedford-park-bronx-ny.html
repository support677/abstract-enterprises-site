Riser. The apartment stock’s vertical spine. One failed span silences the floors above it; the trace finds the span, the splice ends it.

Shared component. The amplifier, supply or board every line passes through. All-lines symptoms point here, not at the stations.

Release path. Button to strike, end to end, in either era. Visitors heard but never admitted means the fault lives here and nowhere else.

Station. The in-unit wall unit, any generation. Fails alone, replaced alone — the riser keeps ringing around it.

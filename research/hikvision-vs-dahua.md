- Firecrawl search snippet · https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/06/2026-13518/prohibiting-importation-and-marketing-of-previously-authorized-covered-communications-equipment (Federal Register notice published 07/06/2026, DA 26-635, FR ID 354261) · "consumers may continue to use any device or equipment that they currently possess, if the equipment was legally purchased and maintains an existing equipment authorization"; "Allowing imports and marketing replacements for existing covered equipment would defeat the entire purpose of this prohibition." · used: federal section (publication date July 6, 2026; page says the prohibition took effect in mid-July 2026, i.e. 10 days after publication, without inventing an exact day).

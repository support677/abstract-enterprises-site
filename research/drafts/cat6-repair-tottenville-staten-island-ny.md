**Thesis (differs from parent = water line / houses / Sandy; St. George = inherited office cabling; soundbar Tottenville = wall eras for TV mounting):** almost no Tottenville house was built for Ethernet — 20% of homes predate 1940 (balloon-framed village houses with knee walls and porches) and 55% were built 1980–2009 with phone and coax only — so the Cat6 in them is retrofit cable laid along the quickest route (open stud bays, knee-wall spaces, garage ceilings beside the opener rail, attics over garages, down the outside of the house, inside gutter downspouts, through boxes screwed to vinyl siding). Faults collect at those shortcuts, so the repair is about finding and re-routing the shortcut, not just re-terminating the ends. Flood-line content is left to the parent page (one paragraph and one bullet only).

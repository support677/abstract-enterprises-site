## Distinct theses

**Port Morris** — the only commercial page in the silo. Converted industrial and multi-tenant buildings where a failed entrance stops deliveries and has a running daily cost. Covers the pedestrian door versus the freight door as separate problems, stations nobody can hear across a large floor plate, and the third option that usually wins here: *adding* a station rather than repairing or replacing one.

**Schuylerville** — garden apartment complexes where each entrance runs its own complete system, all installed in the same year and ageing on the same schedule. The commercial argument is arithmetic: the three-hour minimum applies per visit, not per entrance, so assessing several together costs a fraction of meeting them as successive emergencies.

**Silver Beach** — a private waterfront community where two non-technical factors govern the work: community arrangements about anything touching the exterior or shared land, and narrow-lane access that has to be planned at the booking. Salt corrosion is present but deliberately secondary, since Locust Point already leads on it.

Video Intercoms & Access Control

This category covers video intercom kits and stations from Akuvox, Dahua, Uniview, and Comelit, plus access readers, proximity tags, and electric lock accessories. Items include door phones, keypad units, touchscreen indoor panels, monitor stands, and flush-mount boxes.

Confirm whether the intercom system is IP or analog before you buy, since IP stations connect to your network and need PoE or separate power. Check that the lock type, such as an electric strike or magnetic lock, matches the hardware you select.

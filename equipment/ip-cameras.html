IP Security Cameras

This category covers network cameras from Hikvision, Uniview, Dahua, and Everest, including turret, bullet, dome, eyeball, and fisheye models. Resolutions run from 4MP to 8MP, and many models offer IR night viewing while some provide 360-degree panoramic views.

Lens focal length controls field of view, so a 2.8mm lens sees a wider area than a 4mm or 6mm lens mounted at the same spot. Choose the lens based on how far the subject is from the camera and how wide an area you need to cover.

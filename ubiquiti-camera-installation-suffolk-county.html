Ubiquiti Questions Suffolk Owners Ask

Cost

How much does Ubiquiti camera installation cost in Suffolk County?

Expect roughly $480–$850 per camera installed in Suffolk including the NVR, PoE cabling, configuration, and the UniFi Protect app setup — above NYC because of larger lots and longer runs, with the Hamptons and East End running higher still. A full home perimeter often lands around $3,200–$6,500; estates and Hamptons properties run higher. Final pricing follows a free site visit. Call (631) 407-2884.

Why does a Suffolk property cost more to cover?

Bigger lots and longer runs. Suffolk has the largest lots on Long Island — long driveways, multiple outbuildings, pools, fields — so it’s often 8–20 cameras with serious cable. Ubiquiti’s value is exactly why it’s popular for covering that much ground affordably. Beware a contractor who quotes 50 cameras and a $30k system before walking the property.

Quality / Trust

Can you secure a Hamptons or seasonal home?

Yes — this is a big part of our Suffolk work. We set up full coverage with the UniFi Protect app remote monitoring and UniFi AI alerts so you can watch a vacant East End or North Fork home all off-season from anywhere, with real alerts on actual activity, not noise.

Can you cover a winery, farm, or marina?

Yes — North Fork wineries, horse farms, and marinas all need perimeter monitoring over wide open ground. We use PTZ to cover fields and lots, LPR at entrances, and high-channel NVRs, with weatherproof cameras throughout.

DIY vs Pro

Can I install Ubiquiti myself on my Suffolk property?

You can mount a small kit, but a large Suffolk property is where DIY breaks down: long cable runs across acres to outbuildings, aiming many cameras with no blind spots, weatherproofing for the coast, sizing NVR storage, and securing the system against the remote-access exploits Ubiquiti gear is targeted for. A pro gets it right the first time.

Which Ubiquiti cameras are best for a big Suffolk lot?

Usually weatherproof UniFi G5 turret or bullet cameras with UniFi AI at the perimeter and entrances, plus PTZ for a long driveway, grounds, or field, all on a right-sized NVR. We map placement to eliminate blind spots on a free walk-through.

Technical

Why is my Ubiquiti camera offline or not recording?

Usually a PoE/power fault on a long run, a full or failed NVR drive, a network conflict, or firmware. “Offline” is often the camera losing its link to the NVR; “not recording” is usually storage. We diagnose and fix it — often in one visit, on systems we didn’t install.

Wired or wireless for a large Suffolk property?

Wired PoE is more reliable for permanent coverage and what we recommend for most homes. On very large estates and farms where a run to a distant barn or outbuilding isn’t practical, a reliable wireless or mesh link can fill the gap. We advise per property.

Commercial / Compliance

Is Ubiquiti legal to install in Suffolk County?

Yes, for private use. US restrictions (the NDAA and related FCC rules) bar Ubiquiti from federal government and certain federally-funded or critical-infrastructure projects — not from private homes, estates, wineries, retail, or commercial properties. If your property is government or federally-funded, tell us and we’ll spec a compliant brand instead.

Can cameras avoid recording a neighbor’s property?

Yes — we aim cameras to cover your property and entrances and use privacy masking where helpful. On Suffolk’s larger lots there’s usually plenty of room to cover your grounds without pointing at a neighbor, and we handle it deliberately.

“Is Ubiquiti Banned?” — The Honest Answer

US law — the NDAA and related FCC rules — restricts Ubiquiti from federal government use and certain federally-funded and critical-infrastructure projects. It is not banned for private use, and it’s installed legally across countless Suffolk homes, estates, wineries, and businesses.

For a private Suffolk property it’s legal and appropriate. If your property is a public school, a government office, or federally funded, we won’t install it — we’ll spec a compliant brand. A contractor who tells you that up front is worth more than a spec sheet that hides it.

Plain-English Glossary

Ubiquiti Terminology, Explained

UniFi G5

Ubiquiti’s full-color night vision — a wide aperture and warm supplement light capture color 24/7 instead of black-and-white infrared.

UniFi AI

On-camera AI that classifies people and vehicles versus other motion, cutting false alarms and labeling footage so it’s searchable.

NVR

Network Video Recorder — records all cameras, powers them over PoE, and serves footage to your phone. The heart of the system.

PoE

Power over Ethernet — one cable carries power and data to each camera, ideal for the long runs a large Suffolk lot needs.

IP66 / IP67

Weatherproof ratings — dust-tight and protected against heavy rain, essential for Suffolk’s coastal and waterfront exterior cameras.

PTZ

Pan-Tilt-Zoom — a motorized dome that moves and zooms to cover a long driveway, estate grounds, or a vineyard from one point.

LPR / ANPR

License Plate Recognition — reads and logs plates with timestamps at a gated driveway, winery, or commercial entrance.

the UniFi Protect app

The app for live view, playback, and alerts on your phone — ideal for seasonal homes, no monthly fee for local recording.

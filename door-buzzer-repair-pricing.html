What’s Covered in the $250 Service Call

✓ Covered
🔌

Broken or Loose Wire

Disconnected, frayed, or loose wiring at the buzzer panel, intercom handset, or door strike. We reconnect and test.

✓ Covered
🧹

Corroded Terminal

Oxidized or corroded wire terminals causing intermittent buzzer failure. We clean, strip, and reterminate.

✓ Covered
⚡

Blown Fuse or Tripped Breaker

Buzzer system not getting power due to a blown fuse or tripped low-voltage breaker. Quick swap and restore.

✓ Covered
🔘

Faulty Button or Switch

Worn-out or stuck buzzer button at the front door or lobby panel. We replace the button on-site.

✓ Covered
📞

Loose Handset Connection

Interior handset not ringing or producing sound due to a loose plug or disconnected jack. Reconnect and test.

✓ Covered
🔋

Dead Battery (Wireless Systems)

Wireless buzzer not working due to dead batteries. We replace and verify signal strength.

What If It’s a Bigger Job?

If the repair requires new wiring, a full panel replacement, or a system upgrade, your $250 goes directly toward the total cost of the job. We give you a clear quote on-site before doing any additional work. No surprises, no wasted money.

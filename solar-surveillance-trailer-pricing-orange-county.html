Why Orange Pricing Lands Here

Three real factors shape the Orange baseline versus the Brooklyn/NYC entry rate of $2,500/mo.

1. Bronx-to-Orange tow distance

70 minutes to Newburgh via Thruway Exit 17, 90+ to Middletown and Goshen, 110+ to Port Jervis and Western Orange. The +30% rate reflects the long-tow round-trip on every service visit.

2. Winter spec & LiFePO₄ inclusion

Hudson Valley winters bottom out below 5°F multiple nights per year. LiFePO₄ + heated battery enclosure is standard, not an upsell. Cheap AGM units that drive the $800 national floor lose 30–40% capacity at those temps and fail.

3. ARB & historic-district handling

Active LPC/ARB districts in Orange include Newburgh HD, Goshen Village HD, Warwick Village HD, Pine Bush Village. Orange deployments require LPC-aware placement and occasionally neutral wraps — extra prep that doesn't apply on every job. The Orange tier reflects that handling time when applicable.

3. “My stone veneer fireplace — drill into stone or studs?”

Both, and this distinction is critical. Stone veneer is thin (typically 1-2 inches) applied over wood or metal studs. The best approach is to drill through the veneer into the studs behind for maximum holding power. Drilling only into veneer risks cracking the stone and provides insufficient hold for anything over 40 lbs. Full-depth real stone (common in older Putnam County colonials built before 1990) has no studs behind it — you need sleeve anchors or wedge anchors rated for the combined weight of your TV and mount. We always tap-test to determine veneer vs. full-depth stone, then pilot drill to confirm depth before committing to full-size anchors. Getting this wrong is how people crack their stone and end up calling us for rescue.

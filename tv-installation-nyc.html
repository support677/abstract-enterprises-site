TV Mount Types: Wall Mount Bracket Installation, Tilt Mount, Full Motion Mount

Every TV bracket installation starts with choosing the right mount for your room layout, viewing angle, wall type, and TV size. Here are the mount types we install with mounting hardware installation included:

Fixed / Flat Mount — Wall Mount Bracket Installation

Sits flush against the wall with the slimmest profile. The most affordable TV mounting option for eye-level flat screen installation where the viewing angle won't change. Includes all mounting hardware installation and stud mounting.

Tilt Mount Installation

Allows 10-15 degrees downward tilt. Perfect for TV mounting above fireplace NYC, bedroom installs, or kitchen viewing. Reduces glare from overhead lights and windows. Tilt mount installation is our most popular upgrade for above-eye-level positions.

Full Motion Mount Installation

Extends, swivels, and tilts in all directions. Best for open floor plans, corner installations, and rooms where you watch from multiple seating areas. Full motion mount installation requires solid stud mounting or masonry anchoring for safety.

Ceiling Mount

Drops the TV from the ceiling on an adjustable pole. Used in lofts with exposed ceilings, commercial spaces like restaurant TV installation and bar TV mounting setups, and rooms where wall mounting isn't possible.

TV Mounting Above Fireplace NYC

Specialized installation with heat-resistant considerations. We verify clearance from the firebox, use a tilting or pull-down mount, and anchor into brick or stone with masonry-rated hardware. TV mounting above fireplace is one of our most requested services.

Samsung Frame TV Installation — OLED TV Installation

Art-style TVs like Samsung Frame TV installation require a flush, no-gap mount and recessed One Connect box. OLED TV installation for LG Gallery series uses ultra-slim wall mounts. We handle the specialty mounting hardware installation these premium TVs require.

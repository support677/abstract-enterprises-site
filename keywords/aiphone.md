## Rules for these pages

- **Slug: `aiphone-intercom-<area>`.** One page per area covering Aiphone repair, replacement, upgrades, maintenance AND installation. Aiphone is mostly a REPAIR and UPGRADE brand in NYC (GT Series in thousands of multi-tenant buildings, JP/JO/JK/LEF in houses and offices), so lead with repair and upgrade intent.
- **Cannibalization (BLUEPRINT §12.0):** no aiphone-repair-* page exists, so these pages carry the Aiphone-specific repair and not-working phrases. The "Semantic / related" section is supporting context only: those generic phrases belong to the intercom-installation-*, intercom-repair-* and door-buzzer-repair-* pages, so they may appear in body sentences but never in an Aiphone page's title, meta, H1/H2/H3 or FAQ questions. Every Aiphone page links once in context to its area's intercom repair, door buzzer repair and intercom installation pages; the builder adds one contextual link back from the area's intercom repair page.
- **Prices:** only what's already on the site. Intercom service call $250 diagnostic (the area's service-call rate per [[aess-pricing]] where it differs), door buzzer part ranges exactly as on that area's door-buzzer-repair page, installation = written quote after the site visit. Never invent Aiphone equipment prices; never compare to a retail or manufacturer store price.
- **Dealer / authorized / certified phrases:** Abstract Enterprises Security Systems is NOT an Aiphone authorized dealer or certified installer. Never claim it ("Aiphone dealer locator" is answered by saying we install and service Aiphone; we are not listed as a dealer). Only Lorex, Luminys and Reolink may be called partners.
- **Navigational phrases:** never imply we are Aiphone. Manuals, the app account, dealer locator, warranty and manufacturer support are Aiphone's; building hardware, wiring, door release, power supply and programming problems are ours. Never reproduce manuals or wiring diagrams.
- **Product lines and model numbers:** every series, model and feature claim (GT, IX, IXG, JO, JP, JK, JF, AC Series / AC Nio, LEF, KB, IXG app/cloud, subscriptions, wiring compatibility) must be confirmed on aiphone.com or its datasheets (Firecrawl) before use. Drop any that can't be confirmed, and don't state which models are discontinued unless Aiphone says so.
- **How-to / why phrases:** explain what's happening and what a resident or super can safely check (app, phone settings, whether the whole building or one unit is down), then book the tech. No step-by-step wiring, programming or power-supply testing. Building repairs need access to every affected unit on the visit.
- **Same-day / today / emergency phrases (incl. the voice phrase "fix my Aiphone door buzzer today"):** answer honestly. We book within 2 hours, schedule 7 days a week with early-day visits, first come first served once paid. Never promise same-day repair, never "24/7".
- **Comparison phrases:** honest installer's view per BLUEPRINT §12.5; link existing vs-pages (/akuvox-vs-butterflymx, /doorbird-vs-2n). No new vs-pages without confirmed demand. Never knock a competitor company.
- **Integration phrases:** only what Aiphone documents. We don't do electrical work: the low-voltage and door hardware side is ours; any new high-voltage circuit is the building's electrician.
- **Warranty:** Aiphone's manufacturer warranty is theirs; our labor warranty is the 30-day warranty on the site; 1-year and longer are paid add-ons.
- **Reviews / pros and cons:** honest installer's view; only our real Google rating. No invented testimonials.
- **Seasonal phrases:** FAQ or section angles where they fit the area; don't force them all onto every page.
- **Area phrases only on their own area's page.** NYC hub may use the five borough phrases; Long Island hub Nassau/Suffolk; Hudson Valley hub its six counties.
- Never write "AESS" on the page; always "Abstract Enterprises Security Systems". Company byline only. Real intercom job photos only; Aiphone product shots labeled as product shots.

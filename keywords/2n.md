- **Prices:** only what's already on the site (intercom installation ranges as shown on that area's intercom-installation page, intercom service call $250 diagnostic or the area's rate per [[aess-pricing]], installation quote after the site visit). Never invent 2N equipment or My2N licence prices; never compare to a retail or manufacturer store price.
- **Dealer / authorized / certified / partner phrases:** do NOT claim 2N dealer, authorized, certified or partner status unless Anwar confirms it in writing. Say we install, program and service 2N. Only Lorex, Luminys and Reolink may be called partners.
- **Navigational phrases** (My2N, 2N Mobile Video, app, login, manuals, firmware): never imply we are 2N. Accounts, licences, the app and manufacturer support are 2N's; building hardware, wiring, door release, network and programming are ours. Never reproduce manuals, wiring diagrams or firmware.
- **Products / features** (2N Mobile Video, My2N, fingerprint/biometric reader, answering units, IP Verso / IP Style / IP Solo / Indoor Touch etc. if they appear in the rest of the list): confirm every model and feature on 2n.com or its datasheets with Firecrawl before use; drop any that can't be confirmed.
- **How-to / why phrases:** explain what's going on and what a manager can safely check, then book the tech. No step-by-step wiring, SIP programming or reset procedures. Building repairs need access to every affected unit on the visit.
- **Same-day / emergency / 24-hour / weekend / remote-support phrases:** answer honestly. We book within 2 hours, schedule 7 days a week with early-day visits, first come first served once paid. Never promise same-day work, never "24/7". Free tech support for our customers; never promise remote repair.
- **We don't do electrical work:** the low-voltage, PoE and door hardware side is ours; any new high-voltage circuit is the building's electrician.
- **Warranty:** 2N's manufacturer warranty is theirs; our labor warranty is the 30-day warranty on the site; 1-year and longer are paid add-ons.
- **Area phrases only on their own area's page.** Never write "AESS"; always "Abstract Enterprises Security Systems". Company byline only. Real 2N job photos only if we have them; product shots labeled as product shots.

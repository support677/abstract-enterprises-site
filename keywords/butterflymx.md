## Rules for these pages

- **One page per brand per area, installation AND repair (Anwar, Nov 24 2026).** There is no butterflymx-repair-* page, so per BLUEPRINT §12.0 the ButterflyMX pages carry the ButterflyMX-specific repair, not-working, replacement and maintenance phrases. Generic intercom repair wording ("intercom repair Brooklyn", "buzzer not working") stays on the intercom-repair-* and door-buzzer-repair-* pages. Every ButterflyMX page links once in context to its area's intercom repair page and intercom installation page, and to /akuvox-vs-butterflymx; the builder adds one contextual link back from the area's intercom installation page.
- **NYC hub already exists (thin, noindex).** /butterflymx-installation-nyc is an old 1,368-word page with `noindex`. Rebuild it IN PLACE to the full blueprint (same URL, same slug; never rename or delete), and remove `noindex` only once it passes tools/check_page.py.
- **Area phrases go only on their own area's page** (the list's "ButterflyMX installation Brooklyn" / "ButterflyMX repair Westchester County" … lines). The NYC hub may use the five borough phrases; the Long Island hub Nassau/Suffolk; the Hudson Valley hub its six counties.
- **Prices:** only what's already on the site. Installation = written quote after the site visit; hardware and the yearly software subscription are set by ButterflyMX; intercom service call $250 diagnostic (the area's service-call rate per [[aess-pricing]] where it differs). Cost keywords (cost per unit / per apartment, monthly fee, subscription cost) are answered by explaining those parts honestly; never invent ButterflyMX prices or ranges. We price a no-subscription alternative (Akuvox) beside every ButterflyMX quote. Never compare to a retail or manufacturer store price.
- **Dealer / certified / authorized / partner phrases:** Abstract Enterprises Security Systems is NOT a ButterflyMX certified, authorized or official dealer/partner. Never claim it. Say we install, wire, integrate and service ButterflyMX systems. Only Lorex, Luminys and Reolink may be called partners. "Buy ButterflyMX / for sale / supplier / wholesale" phrases: we supply and install the system as part of the job.
- **Navigational phrases** (login, resident/admin portal, dashboard, app download, official website, customer service, support phone number, help center, manuals): never imply we are ButterflyMX. Answer plainly: account logins, the app and subscription billing are handled by ButterflyMX itself; for hardware, wiring, door release, network or panel problems in the building, call us. No manuals or wiring diagrams reproduced.
- **How-to phrases** (how to install / set up / program / reset / connect to WiFi / add residents / create virtual keys / troubleshoot): explain what's involved and what a building manager can do in the ButterflyMX dashboard, but no step-by-step DIY wiring, programming or troubleshooting (we don't hand out how-to before a paid job). Real facts only, pulled from ButterflyMX's own pages with Firecrawl.
- **Same-day / emergency / 24-hour / weekend / after-hours phrases:** answer honestly. We book within 2 hours, schedule 7 days a week with early-day visits, first come first served once paid. Never promise same-day installation or repair, never "24/7 service".
- **Competitor brand "vs" phrases:** compare honestly per BLUEPRINT §12.5 and link to the existing vs-pages (/akuvox-vs-butterflymx, /doorbird-vs-2n). Don't build new vs-pages from this list without confirming demand. Never knock a competitor company.
- **Integration phrases** (Yardi, Entrata, RealPage, AppFolio, Brivo, HID, elevator, electric strike, maglock): only what ButterflyMX documents. We don't do electrical work: say the door hardware and low-voltage side are ours, any new high-voltage circuit is the building's electrician.
- **Reviews / complaints / pros and cons:** honest installer's view; only real Google reviews for our own rating. No invented testimonials, no quoting third-party reviews.
- **Warranty phrases:** ButterflyMX's own hardware warranty is theirs; our labor warranty is the 30-day warranty on the site; 1-year and longer are paid add-ons (BLUEPRINT warranty line).
- **Seasonal phrases** (winter problems, weather/water/storm damage, power outage, package theft, holiday delivery access, summer/year-end installs): good FAQ or section angles, used where they fit the area; don't force all of them onto every page.
- Never write "AESS" on the page; always "Abstract Enterprises Security Systems". Company byline only. Real intercom job photos only (no AI images, no ButterflyMX marketing photos passed off as our jobs; product shots labeled as product shots).

## Rules for these pages

- **Don't cannibalize the home automation silo.** The 16 existing home-automation-installation-* pages stay the money pages for "home automation [area]" wording (the daily GSC optimizer works the home automation × area phrases from this list into those pages; never change their titles, H1s or URLs). Smart home pages target the "smart home" wording. Each smart home page links once in context to its area's home automation, home theater, TV installation, Sonos, smart lock, doorbell camera, security camera, alarm, home security system, network/structured cabling and low-voltage/thermostat wiring pages where they exist, and gets one contextual link back from each.
- **Photos:** use Anwar's own smart home job photos (uploaded Nov 2026 for the smart home hubs and county pages), with alt text. No stock, no AI images.
- **Brands — installs, not certification.** Abstract Enterprises Security Systems installs Lutron, Control4, Crestron and Savant (parts bought through authorized dealers) but is NOT brand-certified yet. Never say "certified", "authorized dealer" or "official partner" for those four. "Licensed" means our NYS low-voltage contractor license only. "Certified smart home installers" is a search phrase to answer, not a claim. Official partners we can say: Lorex, Luminys, Reolink. Other brands on the list (Alexa, Google Home/Nest, Apple HomeKit, SmartThings, Home Assistant, Sonos, UniFi, Philips Hue, Ecobee, Ring, Aqara, Shelly, TP-Link/Kasa/Tapo, Leviton, Legrand, Brilliant, Alarm.com, Qolsys, Honeywell/Resideo, 2GIG): use for compatibility, comparison and integration content.
- **Out of scope: answer the question, never sell the work.** We don't do electrical work, plumbing, appliance install or wall patching. So smart electrical panels, smart circuit breakers, EV chargers, solar, smart water heaters, toilets, showers, faucets, water shutoff valves, irrigation/sprinklers, pool automation and kitchen/laundry appliances get mentioned only as things a smart home system can tie into (the customer's electrician or plumber installs the device; we integrate and program it). Line-voltage switches and dimmers: say we install and program the lighting control system; don't promise electrical/panel work.
- **Ask Anwar before selling as our service:** motorized shades/blinds/curtains, smart garage/gate openers and driveway gates, medical alert / fall detection. Until he confirms, cover them as integrations only.
- **Smoke / CO / fire:** smart smoke and CO detectors only as alarm-panel add-on sensors. No fire alarm system claims (fire alarm pages are separate and Suffolk / Hudson Valley only).
- **Monitoring and fees:** alarm monitoring tiers are only $45/month (app) and $30/month (keypad). "No monthly fee / no subscription / without internet / local control" phrases get honest answers (many lighting, audio and local-control systems run without a subscription; alarm monitoring is the paid part). Never claim a $0 monitored plan.
- **Prices:** only prices Anwar has set that are already on the site. "Under $100…$10000", "financing", "payment plans", "no upfront cost", "free estimates / free consultations" only if Anwar confirms the offer; otherwise explain what drives cost and point to the quote form. TV/device programming is charged at the area hourly rate (Oct 13 2026 rule).
- **No same-day promises.** Seasonal phrases (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas gifts) only in a dated tips/ideas section, never a fake sale.
- **"Buy / shop / for sale / store / wholesale / kits"** phrases: link to equipment store pages where they exist (equipment-sales plan); otherwise "buy the system installed." No DIY install how-tos for leads.
- **Audiences** (builders, developers, architects, interior designers, property managers, landlords, Airbnb hosts): fold into sections/FAQs on these pages; dedicated audience pages only if Anwar asks.
- Always "Abstract Enterprises Security Systems", never "AESS". No Anwar byline.

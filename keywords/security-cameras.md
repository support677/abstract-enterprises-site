Never change those pages' titles, H1s or URLs.
- **Prices — never invent one.** Installed prices: only the area's starting price that tools/check_page.py takes from security-camera-pricing-<area> (answer-first sentence, e.g. "buy equipment only, or have it installed from $X"). - **No checkout yet.** There is no store checkout on the site. CTAs are the regular hero form, sticky bar, call and text. Don't write "add to cart", "order online", "ships today" or "free shipping".
- **Delivery yes, pickup no (Anwar, Nov 20 2026).** We deliver equipment to the customer — "call or text and we'll deliver it to your home or business in <area>." There is no pickup counter: answer "pickup / local pickup / same day pickup" phrases by offering delivery instead. Never claim NDAA, ONVIF, IP67, IK10, H.265, LPR, facial recognition or AI detection for a product unless its spec sheet says so.
- **Wholesale / trade / bulk / distributor / supplier phrases.** Installers, property managers and businesses buying in quantity call or text for a trade quote. No trade price lists, no tier talk, no "lowest price guaranteed".
- **Reviews / ratings / "top rated".** Only real Google reviews and the rating/review count check_page.py enforces. Never write testimonials. "Best / top rated" phrases are answered with how to choose (buying guide content), not superlatives about ourselves.
- **Comparisons (wired vs wireless, PoE vs WiFi, NVR vs DVR, 4K vs 2K, IP vs analog, cloud vs local).** Comparison tables with check marks are good AI-citation content — real facts only. Camera quotes go out as a 2K and a 4K option, so present both.
- **Laws / privacy / regulations phrases.** Real sourced facts only (NY Penal Law §250 on recording conversations, NYC's biometric identifier signage law for commercial establishments, landlord/tenant rules), cited. No legal advice.
- **How-to phrases** (how to choose, calculate storage, view on a phone, prevent hacking): explain what's involved and what to look for; storage math is fine (it's a buying question). No step-by-step DIY wiring or troubleshooting.
- **Monthly fees / subscription phrases.** Our camera systems record locally with no monthly fee; cloud storage only where the product actually offers it.
- **Warranty.** The 30-day warranty on the site; 1-year and longer are paid add-ons (exact line per BLUEPRINT). Equipment bought without installation carries the manufacturer's warranty.

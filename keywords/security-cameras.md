- **What the page is.** A local store page: the security cameras, NVR/DVR recorders, surveillance hard drives, PoE switches, mounts, junction boxes and cable Abstract Enterprises Security Systems sells in that area, delivered to the customer. Product sections are organized the way buyers search (outdoor/indoor, 4K/8MP/5MP/2K, PoE/WiFi/wired, dome/bullet/turret/PTZ, recorders and storage, accessories, packages by property type and business type). Being local is the edge over Amazon: a store that matches the recorder to the cameras and helps with phone viewing.

Never compare our prices to a retail or manufacturer store price. Cost / price keywords are answered with what drives the price (resolution, camera count, recorder channels, storage days, PoE vs WiFi).
- **No checkout yet.** CTAs are the regular hero form, sticky bar, call and text. Don't write "add to cart", "order online", "ships today" or "free shipping".
- **Delivery yes, pickup no (Anwar, Nov 20 2026).** "Call or text and we'll deliver it to your home or business in <area>." No pickup counter: answer pickup phrases by offering delivery. Don't promise same-day delivery, free delivery, a delivery fee or a delivery window.

- **Product facts.** Real product photos only and real specs from the manufacturer page. Never claim NDAA, ONVIF, IP67, IK10, H.265, LPR, facial recognition or AI detection unless the spec sheet says so.
- **Wholesale / trade / bulk phrases.** Installers, property managers and businesses buying in quantity call or text for a trade quote. No trade price lists, no tier talk, no "lowest price guaranteed".
- **Warranty.** Equipment carries the manufacturer's warranty.
- **Seasonal phrases** (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, holiday package theft, winter-rated outdoor cameras, vacation homes): good FAQ / section angles where they fit the area. Only deals or promos Anwar has set — never invent a discount, a sale price or a countdown.
- Never write "AESS" on the page — always "Abstract Enterprises Security Systems". Company byline only. Credentials: "Licensed NYS low-voltage contractor", insured.

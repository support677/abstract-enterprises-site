## Rules for these pages

- **Slug: `comelit-intercom-<area>`** (e.g. /comelit-intercom-nyc, /comelit-intercom-brooklyn). The page covers Comelit installation, repair, replacement, upgrades and maintenance. Comelit is mostly a REPAIR and UPGRADE brand in NYC (thousands of existing Simplebus / Simplebus 2 / ViP buildings), so lead with repair, not-working and upgrade intent, then installation.
- **One page per brand per area, installation AND repair (Anwar, Nov 24 2026).** No comelit-repair-* page exists, so per BLUEPRINT §12.0 these pages carry the Comelit-specific repair, troubleshooting, replacement and maintenance phrases. Generic phrases (the "Context only" section at the bottom: apartment intercom repair, door buzzer repair, apartment buzzer replacement, intercom maintenance contract …) are owned by the intercom-installation-*, intercom-repair-* and door-buzzer-repair-* pages: they may appear in body sentences for context but NEVER in a Comelit page's title, meta, H1/H2/H3 or FAQ questions. Every Comelit page links once in context to its area's intercom repair, door buzzer repair and intercom installation pages; the builder adds one contextual link back from the area's intercom repair page.
- **Prices:** only what's already on the site. Intercom service call $250 diagnostic (the area's service-call rate per [[aess-pricing]] where it differs), door buzzer repair part ranges exactly as they appear on that area's door-buzzer-repair page, installation = written quote after the site visit. Cost / price-per-apartment / price list / kit phrases are answered by explaining what drives the price; never invent Comelit equipment prices. We can price an Akuvox / DoorBird alternative beside a Comelit replacement quote. Never compare to a retail or manufacturer store price.
- **Dealer / authorized / certified / distributor phrases:** Abstract Enterprises Security Systems is NOT a Comelit authorized dealer, distributor or certified installer. Never claim it. Say we install, repair, program and service Comelit systems and source parts through our suppliers. Only Lorex, Luminys and Reolink may be called partners. "Buy / for sale / online store / wholesale / replacement parts for sale" phrases: we supply parts as part of a repair or install job; no store, no checkout.
- **Navigational phrases** (Comelit website, USA website, login, app download, customer service number, support center, manuals, wiring diagrams, datasheets, firmware/software download, price list, catalog): never imply we are Comelit. Logins, the app account and manufacturer support are Comelit's; building hardware, wiring, door release, power supply and programming problems are ours. Never reproduce manuals, wiring diagrams or firmware.
- **Model numbers and product lines** (Simplebus, Simplebus 2, Simplebus Top, ViP, ViP Manager, Ultra, Ultra Touch, 3one6, Ikall, Quadra, Mini, Maxi, Icona, Planux, Visto, HFX-7000M/9000M, 2602, 2608, 2702W, 2708W, 6741W, 6721W, 6701W, 6601W, 1209, 1210, 4888C, 1440, 1406, 1409, 1404, 1414): only facts confirmed on Comelit's own pages or datasheets (pull with Firecrawl). If a model number can't be confirmed as a real Comelit part, leave it out. Real product photos labeled as product photos; real AESS intercom job photos for job claims.
- **How-to / why phrases** (how to program apartment numbers, reset, wire, connect the app, why is my Comelit buzzing/not ringing/screen black): explain what's going on and what a resident or super can safely check (app, Wi-Fi, a monitor setting), then book the tech. No step-by-step wiring, programming or power-supply testing (we don't hand out how-to before a paid job). Building repairs need access to every affected unit on the visit.
- **Same-day / emergency / 24-hour / weekend / after-hours phrases:** answer honestly. We book within 2 hours, schedule 7 days a week with early-day visits, first come first served once paid. Never promise same-day installation or repair, never "24/7 service".
- **Competitor "vs" / alternative phrases:** honest installer's view per BLUEPRINT §12.5; link to existing vs-pages (/akuvox-vs-butterflymx, /doorbird-vs-2n). Don't build new vs-pages from this list without confirmed demand. Never knock a competitor company.
- **Integration phrases** (electric strike, maglock, elevator, gate, CCTV, SIP/VoIP, PoE, smart lock): only what Comelit documents. We don't do electrical work: the low-voltage and door hardware side is ours; any new high-voltage circuit is the building's electrician.
- **Warranty phrases:** Comelit's manufacturer warranty is theirs; our labor warranty is the 30-day warranty on the site; 1-year and longer are paid add-ons (BLUEPRINT warranty line). Don't promise "Comelit warranty repair" handling beyond that.
- **Reviews / complaints / pros and cons:** honest installer's view; only our real Google rating. No invented testimonials, no quoting third-party reviews.
- **Seasonal / damage phrases** (water, weather, storm damage, power outage, winter problems, annual inspection, renovation): good FAQ or section angles where they fit the area.
- **Area phrases only on their own area's page.** NYC hub may use the five borough phrases; Long Island hub Nassau/Suffolk; Hudson Valley hub its six counties.
- Never write "AESS" on the page; always "Abstract Enterprises Security Systems". Company byline only.

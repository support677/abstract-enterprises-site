TV Mount Types: Wall Mount Bracket Installation, Tilt Mount, Full Motion Mount

Every TV bracket installation starts with choosing the right mount. Here are the mount types we install across the Hudson Valley with mounting hardware installation included:

Fixed / Flat Mount — Wall Mount Bracket Installation

Sits flush against the wall. The most affordable TV mounting option for eye-level flat screen installation. Includes all mounting hardware installation and stud mounting. Perfect for living rooms and bedrooms in Hudson Valley homes.

Tilt Mount Installation

Allows 10-15 degrees downward tilt. Perfect for TV mounting above fireplace — the most popular mount choice for Hudson Valley homes with stone or brick mantels. Reduces glare from the large windows common in suburban homes.

Full Motion Mount Installation

Extends, swivels, and tilts in all directions. Best for open floor plans common in newer Hudson Valley construction, corner installations, and great rooms. Full motion mount installation requires solid stud mounting for safety.

Ceiling Mount

Drops the TV from the ceiling on an adjustable pole. Used in finished basements, man caves, commercial spaces like restaurant TV installation and bar TV mounting setups, and rooms with vaulted ceilings common in Hudson Valley homes.

Ethernet and Cat6 Repair in Williamsburg

Converted factory floors along Kent sit next to mixed-use buildings on Bedford. The typical Williamsburg fault is a crushed Cat6 in a joist bay, a painted-over keystone on a storefront, or a hallway panel that was never labeled when the loft was cut into units.

Williamsburg was annexed to the City of Brooklyn in 1827. The village was laid out by Richard Woodhull and named for Colonel Jonathan Williams. That grid still shows up in the work: long runs down Bedford, short laterals on Berry, and waterfront lofts on Kent that feed cameras facing Domino Park.

SMS / Text Message Consent

Abstract Enterprises Security Systems ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to transparency about how we communicate with you via text message (SMS/MMS).

Consent Language By providing your phone number on any of our website forms, you consent to receive follow-up and informational text messages from Abstract Enterprises Security Systems. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help. Your consent is not a condition of purchase.

What Messages Will I Receive?

When you provide your phone number via our website contact forms, quote request forms, or booking pages, you may receive text messages related to:

  • Appointment confirmations and scheduling updates
  • Service follow-ups and job status notifications
  • Estimate and quote information you requested
  • Responses to your inquiries
  • Informational messages about your service or project

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us and the status of your service request or project. We do not send marketing blasts or promotional spam. Messages are sent in direct response to your inquiry or related to an active service.

Opting Out

You can opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and will no longer receive text messages from us. You may also contact us directly to request removal.

Help

For help or questions about our text messaging, reply HELP to any message or contact us at:

  • Phone: (800) 486-0943
  • Email: openeyesales@gmail.com

Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates from your wireless carrier may apply to any messages you send or receive. Check with your carrier for details on your text messaging plan.

Supported Carriers

Our text messaging services are supported by all major US carriers including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Sprint, and others. Carrier coverage may vary by location.

Privacy

Your phone number and personal information will never be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes. We use your phone number solely to communicate with you about services you have requested or inquired about. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

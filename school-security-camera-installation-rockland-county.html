New York School Camera Law, Read for Rockland

Facial Recognition: Prohibited in Every School

The State Education Department’s September 2023 determination applies to every school in New York without exception — district, charter, Catholic, yeshiva, and independent alike: facial-recognition technology may not be purchased or operated. A cheder in Monsey and the district building in New City answer to the identical sentence. Conventional video — cameras, recording, motion analytics — was never restricted and remains fully lawful. Our installs ship with face-matching capabilities disabled in firmware, the setting logged in the commissioning file, which retires the question whenever a vendor or board member raises it.

Where Lenses May Point — and Where They’re Felonies

Penal Law 250.45 makes recording in bathrooms, mikvah areas, changing rooms, or any space with a reasonable expectation of privacy a felony, with no school exception. The rest of the property is lawful coverage: entrances, corridors, stairs, lunchrooms, gyms, lots, yards, playgrounds, mechanical rooms, perimeter. Classroom and shiur-room interiors we route to your hanhala or board rather than defaulting into; our standing Rockland drawing watches each classroom doorway from the corridor — entries and exits logged, no lens inside the learning space — and any deliberate exception is initialed on the plan. On dormitory properties, coverage stops at residential thresholds, and the drawing documents exactly where and why.

When Footage Becomes a Student Record

FERPA can convert identifiable footage behind a disciplinary outcome into part of that student’s protected file — with parental viewing rights and disclosure limits attached — while Ed Law 2-d in New York attaches enforceable contract obligations to any third party warehousing student-identifiable material. Our default architecture keeps everything clean: the recorder inside your walls, drives entered on the institution’s ledger, a review-and-release protocol handed over in writing at closeout, and duplication off-site permitted solely beneath a 2-d-conforming contract. The eventual hard parent conversation then lands on procedure, not improvisation.

Field Notes: Three Buildings, a Bus Yard, and the 2 A.M. Siphon Crew That Met a New System

Forty-four cameras across a three-building campus in Ramapo, installed through a bein hazmanim window so the heavy work never met a school day. The institution ran a boys’ building, a girls’ building across the lot, a dining and simcha hall behind them — and a bus yard holding twenty-two vehicles that had been bleeding diesel for months, a few gallons at a time, discovered only when the fuel logs stopped reconciling. The existing setup was three orphaned DVRs from three different vendors across three different decades, no two sharing a password, one recording to a drive that had died the previous winter without telling anyone. The administrator’s brief was simple and completely Rockland: one system, one screen, every doorway, and the yard.

We built it as a campus, not three islands. The recorder room went into the main building; the girls’ building joined by point-to-point wireless bridge across the lot — trenching the pavement made no sense — and the dining hall came in on fiber through an existing conduit path a previous electrician had mercifully left labeled. Every entrance got the vestibule treatment the community standardized years ago: face-height lens outside, trap camera inside, live station at each office, release on recognition, with accounts scoped so each building’s office sees its own doors and administration sees everything. Exteriors went in winter-spec — sealed glands, stainless, low-temp housings — because January audits this county’s cut corners annually. The yard got its own architecture: plate capture at the gate, two elevated heads down the rows, and a dedicated motion zone drawn tight around the fuel point, with after-hours person alerts routed to the transportation coordinator’s phone. Commissioning ended at dusk with classification tuning while the local deer made their scheduled appearance along the back fence — and vanished from the alert stream without vanishing from the recording.

Twelve nights after handoff, 2:04 a.m., a sedan rolled past the yard gate slow enough to read — the gate head had its plate before it parked. Two figures walked the bus rows with a can and a hose, settled at the third vehicle, and the fuel-point zone fired: snapshot to the coordinator’s phone, live view open by 2:06. He didn’t drive over; he didn’t need to. The export — plate, faces, four minutes of unhurried work — went to the Rockland County Sheriff’s Office that morning, and the visit that followed ended a leak that had cost the mosad more than the yard cameras did. The fuel logs have reconciled every month since. One campus, one screen, twenty-two buses that now testify for themselves — which was the entire assignment.

Is facial recognition permitted in any Rockland school?

None. Since SED’s September 2023 order, no New York school of any kind — district, charter, Catholic, yeshiva, independent — may buy or run facial recognition. The order never touched regular video: cameras, recorders, and motion analytics stay completely legal. We deliver every system with face-matching switched off at the firmware level and note that state in the commissioning paperwork.

Can cameras go inside classrooms or shiur rooms?

Frequently lawful, never our default. The decision routes to your hanhala, board, and counsel; our standing county drawing covers each classroom doorway from the corridor — entries and exits logged, no lens inside the learning space — and any deliberate in-room exception is initialed on the plan before a bracket goes up.

Which spaces can never be recorded?

Anywhere privacy is reasonably expected: bathrooms, mikvah and changing areas, and dormitory living spaces top the list. Penal Law 250.45 classifies recording in such spaces as a felony, and schools receive no exemption from it. The rest of the property is lawful coverage: entrances, corridors, stairs, lunchrooms, gyms, lots, yards, playgrounds, mechanical rooms, and perimeter, with residential thresholds on dormitory properties drawn precisely on the plan and documented.

We won’t commission below thirty days — complaints and claims in school settings arrive on a delay — and sixty or ninety is available whenever the drive budget stretches. Anything important gets clipped off the recorder right away, permanently beyond the overwrite cycle, and a weakening disk emails an administrator in its first bad week instead of failing silently until footage is demanded.

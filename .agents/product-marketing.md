## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Licensed NYC low-voltage contractor that installs and repairs security cameras, intercoms/door buzzers, access control, alarms, fire alarms and structured cabling across the five boroughs, Long Island and the Hudson Valley.
**What it does:** One company for everything low-voltage in a building — design, wire clean, install, program the app, and support it after. Homes, landlords, co-ops, property managers, stores and commercial buildings. Pay once, own the system (camera systems have no monthly fee; alarm monitoring and intercom app plans are optional monthly add-ons).
**Product category (how people search):** "security camera installation [area]", "door buzzer repair [area]", "intercom installation [area]", "access control installation", "cat6 installation", "fire alarm installation", "TV mounting [area]", "mag lock installation".
**Product type:** Local service business (field install + repair), 5 vans, ~10 crew.
**Business model:** Per-job pricing (equipment + area install rate), deposit to book (no deposit, no appointment), paid in full on negotiated jobs and TV mounting. Recurring: alarm monitoring ($45/mo with app, $30/mo keypad-only), Akuvox app users ($5/user/mo), paid extended warranties (1-year+), CHeKT video monitoring (planned). Financing via Wisetack (HCP). 3% card fee as its own line.
**Legal name:** Abstract Enterprises Security Systems, Inc. — always the full name in anything published. **Never "AESS".**

## Target Audience
**Target customers:**

- Landlords and small multi-unit building owners (3–42 units), co-ops and condos, supers
- Property managers / management companies (e.g. Akam-, Urban Strategies-type portfolios)

- Public / nonprofit / schools (set price only, no negotiation, no card fee)
**Decision-makers:** Owner / homeowner; landlord; property manager or super (often needs board/owner approval); store owner; GC estimator or IT director on commercial.
**Primary use case:** "I need cameras / a working buzzer / an intercom / secure doors, installed right, and someone who answers the phone after."
**Jobs to be done:**
- See and record what happens at my property from my phone (evidence for police/insurance)
- Let tenants/visitors in without me running downstairs — fix the buzzer that's been broken for weeks
- Lock down a door (mag lock + keypad/fob) so only the right people get in
- Wire the building once, clean (Cat6, fiber, PoE) so everything works
**Use cases:** package theft in lobbies, stoop/driveway activity, shoplifting/employee theft, building entry, tenant complaints about dead buzzers, store door control, elevator access control, home theater / TV mounting, video walls.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Security/entry systems that don't work (dead buzzers, offline cameras, cheap DIY kits) and installers who don't show up, wire it ugly, or disappear after the job.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- DIY kits (Ring/Arlo/Wyze/retail): Wi-Fi drops, subscriptions, not built for buildings or concealed wiring
- National alarm companies (ADT/Vivint): long contracts, monthly fees, sales-driven
- Handymen / one-man installers: no license, no insurance, no follow-up support, miss appointments
- Big integrators: slow, expensive, won't touch a 3-unit building or a single buzzer
**What it costs them:** Missed evidence after a break-in, tenant complaints and violations, lost inventory, weeks of waiting, paying twice when a cheap install fails.
**Emotional tension:** Feeling unsafe at home, embarrassment with tenants, being ignored by contractors, fear of getting ripped off on price.

## Objections
| Objection | Response (style: warm, never apologize, reframe to their benefit, hold the rate) |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" / "someone quoted less" | Trim scope first (fewer cameras, less recording, shorter warranty); then the negotiation playbook (tie-down → boss → split). Never undercut the profit floor. |
| "Can you come look first?" | Customer equipment: labor price by phone, no walk-throughs. New construction/bids: send prints first. Others who already have a price can book a visit. |
| "Can I pay later / after?" | No deposit, no appointment. Offer Wisetack financing ("install now, pay later"). |
| "Do I need a monthly plan?" | Cameras: no. Alarm monitoring and app answering are optional add-ons. |
| "What about warranty?" | 30-day warranty included per the website; 1-year+ extended warranties are paid add-ons; tech support is free. |
| "You're a house, not a storefront / not BBB accredited" (1★ review, Oct 2026) | Lead with license #, insurance, 25+ years and 393 reviews; real office plans in progress — don't argue the address. |
| "Your warranty says X but you charged me for service" (1★ reviews, Oct 2026) | State the warranty exactly as written (parts not labor, 30-day standard, paid extended plans) in writing before the sale — no page or flyer may imply more. |
| "Why didn't the tech know what I asked for?" (3★ review, May 2026) | Full scope on the HCP job before dispatch; electrical work (outlets) declined up front. |
Weekly objection report (Quo + Allo transcripts, Mondays) feeds new rows here. **[GAP — call-transcript objections]**

**Anti-persona:** Kitchen hood / fire suppression (Kidde) jobs; electrical work; wall patch/paint; people shopping for parts or how-to advice; Thumbtack leads; tradespeople pulling our prices for their own quotes; anyone who wants a fixed price on unknown hidden cable we didn't install.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Broken buzzer for weeks, camera offline, last installer won't answer, DIY camera keeps dropping Wi-Fi, a break-in or package theft just happened.
**Pull:** One call fixes everything, real license, fast booking, clean concealed install, app set up before the tech leaves, a quote video that shows exactly what they're getting.
**Habit:** "The super will fix it," living with it, the old system "kind of works," contract with ADT.
**Anxiety:** Getting overcharged, holes in walls, techs not showing, paying a deposit to a stranger, special-order delays.

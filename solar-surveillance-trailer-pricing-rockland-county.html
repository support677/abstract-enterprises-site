Why Rockland Pricing Lands Here

Three real factors shape the Rockland baseline versus the Brooklyn/NYC entry rate of $2,500/mo.

1. Bronx-to-Rockland tow distance

40 minutes to Nyack via Mario M. Cuomo Bridge, 50–60 to New City and Spring Valley, 65+ to Suffern and Stony Point. The +25% rate reflects the round-trip on every service visit, including bridge toll absorbed.

2. Winter spec & LiFePO₄ inclusion

Hudson Valley winters bottom out below 5°F multiple nights per year. LiFePO₄ + heated battery enclosure is standard, not an upsell. Cheap AGM units that drive the $800 national floor lose 30–40% capacity at those temps and fail.

3. ARB & historic-district handling

Active LPC/ARB districts in Rockland include Nyack HD, Piermont HD, Stony Point Battlefield (state landmark). Rockland deployments require LPC-aware placement and occasionally neutral wraps — extra prep that doesn't apply on every job. The Rockland tier reflects that handling time when applicable.

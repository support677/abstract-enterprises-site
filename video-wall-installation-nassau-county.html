How do you work around our Westbury catering hall booking calendar?

For active Nassau catering halls, we plan installs in narrow Tuesday/Wednesday windows when most venues have no events. Can you install in a historic 1938 venue without damaging the structure?

Yes. Venues like The Inn at New Hyde Park have specific historic-character constraints — exposed beams, original woodwork, plaster ceilings. We use custom steel adapter brackets that bolt to existing structure without through-bolting historic timber, and never anchor directly to plaster.

Will salt air destroy outdoor installations near Long Island Sound?

Standard outdoor panels with no corrosion protection — yes, you'll see hardware degradation in 18-24 months. Properly spec'd installs use 316-grade stainless steel mounting hardware, marine sealant, and conformal-coated panel electronics. Our North Shore Nassau installs run 6+ years without corrosion failure.

What's the right pixel pitch for a Westbury Manor-scale ballroom?

For 250-400 person ballrooms with viewing distances of 18-35 feet across the floor, 2.5mm to 3.9mm dvLED is the sweet spot. Cocktail hour rooms with closer viewing benefit from 1.9mm pitch.

Can a video wall replace projection in our country club banquet hall?

For most Nassau country clubs, yes — and it should. dvLED walls are 5-10x brighter than even premium projectors, eliminate bulb replacement costs, and don't suffer from washout when you use uplighting. Pays back in 4-6 years just on projector lamp avoidance.

How do you handle Garden City corporate building electrical?

Older Garden City Class B buildings often need a sub-panel added to feed a video wall properly. We coordinate with licensed Nassau electricians for the new circuits, but we spec the load precisely so there's no scope creep mid-install.

Can the wall integrate with our DJ's lighting rig at the catering hall?

Yes. Wireless DMX or Art-Net integration syncs the wall content to lighting cues. Most modern CMS platforms support timeline-based syncing with audio. We install this for multiple Nassau catering halls.

Do we need a video wall in the cocktail hour room and the main ballroom?

Best ROI is a smaller wall (8x5 ft) in cocktail hour and a larger wall (12x7 ft or larger) behind the head table. Cocktail hour is where guests photograph the venue for social media — that content gets shared more than the main ballroom shots.

What electrical do I need for a 4x3 catering hall dvLED wall?

Typically two dedicated 20A 120V circuits plus a separate circuit for controllers and UPS. Larger walls (5x4+) often need 208V three-phase. We coordinate with your Nassau electrician — we don't pull electrical permits, but we spec it precisely.

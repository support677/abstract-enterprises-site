# Security Camera Installation NYC: What It Costs and What to Ask Before You Hire in 2026

Security camera installation NYC quotes can swing widely, and most of that spread comes from the building, not the cameras. After 25+ years wiring cameras into brownstones, walk-ups, storefronts and high-rises across the five boroughs, here is how I'd explain the pricing to a friend, plus the questions worth asking any installer before you sign.

## What a camera system costs in New York City

On our published pricing, a residential system of four to eight cameras usually lands between $850 and $2,500 with the recorder, cabling and installation included. Commercial jobs (retail, restaurants, offices, multi-unit buildings) typically start around $1,500 and can run past $5,000. Three things move the number most: how many cameras you need, how far each cable has to travel, and what your walls are made of. Brick, plaster and concrete take longer to get through cleanly than drywall.

## Why wired beats Wi-Fi in city buildings

Masonry and steel swallow wireless signal between floors, and battery units struggle through a January cold snap. That's why we run PoE cable: a single Ethernet line carries power and video to each camera, so the feed stays steady around the clock. Footage records to a recorder on site, which means no cloud plan and no monthly bill. You own the equipment.

## Five questions to ask any installer

1. Are you licensed by New York State for low-voltage work, and are you insured?
2. Where exactly will the cables run, and will any wiring show on the outside?
3. How many days of footage will the recorder hold?
4. Will you set up the viewing app on my phone before you leave?
5. What warranty comes with the equipment, and what extended coverage can I add?

If an installer can't answer all five clearly, keep looking.

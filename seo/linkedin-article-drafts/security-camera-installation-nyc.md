## Why two quotes for "four cameras" don't match

What moves the price in this city:

- How many floors and walls each cable has to cross to reach the recorder.
- What we mount on: brick, limestone, siding, a drop ceiling or a storefront gate box.
- Resolution and features like color night vision, sirens, strobes and two-way talk.
- How many days of footage you want kept, which sets the drive size.
- Building rules, like a co-op that only allows weekday work.

Brick, concrete and steel are also why we build around wired cameras. Wi-Fi signals die between floors in a pre-war building, and batteries quit in January.

## Questions to ask before you sign

- Are you licensed in New York State for low-voltage work, and can you send a certificate of insurance to my board or managing agent?
- Who keeps the recorder and the admin password when you leave?
- Is there any monthly fee or contract?
- If a camera fails in month two, what is covered and for how long?
- Will any cable show on the outside of the building?

One tip most owners miss: if you also need an intercom, do both in the same visit. They share the same low-voltage paths, and our customers typically save $200 to $400 on labor that way.

Alarm.com installation questions

How much does Alarm.com camera installation cost in Queens?

Four Alarm.com cameras installed in Queens start at $2,077, including mounting, wiring up to 100 ft per camera and setup in the Alarm.com app. Individual Alarm.com cameras and doorbells run $318 to $694 each installed. Recording is through the monthly Alarm.com video monitoring plan, which you pay Abstract Enterprises directly. Prices are plus sales tax.

What is included in the installed price?

The device, mounting, a wire run up to 100 ft (or plug-in power where the model uses it), connection, testing and setup in the Alarm.com app. Hardwired floodlight and spotlight models need 120V power from your electrician; we mount, connect and set them up.

Can you hide the wires?

Yes. Hidden in-wall wiring in hollow drywall is $200 more per camera, up to 100 ft. Spray foam, plaster, brick and exterior walls are checked on site.

Is this an exact quote?

It is an estimate so you know what to expect before we come out. Equipment prices change and every property is different, so your exact price comes after a quick look at the property.

What warranty do I get?

Equipment carries the manufacturer's parts warranty and every install includes our standard 30-day warranty. Our extended warranty, 1, 2 or 3 years, also covers the labor.

I already bought Alarm.com cameras. Will you install them?

Yes. We install customer-supplied cameras priced per line for your area. See our install-your-own pricing.
